1) Short description: Analogue cross-needle meter for indicating the SWR, with scales for the forward and the reflected power and the standing wave ratio.

2) Detailed description: The photo shows an analogue cross-needle meter for indicating the SWR. On the left there is a needle for the forward power ("P_vor") and on the right a needle for the reflected power ("P_rück"). Below the two crossing scales there is a horizontal line with green markings at 1.3, 1.5, 1.6, 1.8, 2, 2.5, 3 and 4. From each of these markings green lines run upwards, inclined up to the left in the left-hand part and up to the right in the right-hand part. The reading is taken from the crossing point of the two needles along the respective green line to the marking on the horizontal line. At the bottom of the meter is the formula S = Umax / Umin.
