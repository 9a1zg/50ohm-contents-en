1) Short description: Neumayer Station in Antarctica; long station building standing on supports, striped white and grey, with a roof section set off in red and with the inscription "Neumayer-Station" on the end face; on the roof several masts, antennas and radomes.

2) Detailed description: The photo shows the German Neumayer Station in Antarctica. The rectangular station building is striped white and grey, has a roof section set off in red and stands raised on many dark supports. On the end face is "Neumayer-Station" in large letters. On the roof there are a red attachment and several whip antennas, mast structures and several white radomes.
