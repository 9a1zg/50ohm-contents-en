1) Short summary:
Table headed "Guide values in transmit mode" ("Richtwerte in Sendemode"), which gives, for several frequency ranges, the required attenuation of unwanted emissions and, as an alternative, the maximum permitted interference powers.

2) Detailed description:
- Column headings: on the left "Frequency range" ("Frequenzbereich"), in the middle "Required attenuation of unwanted emissions relative to the maximum PEP of the transmitter 1)" ("Erforderliche Dämpfung unerwünschter Aussendungen gegenüber der maximalen PEP des Senders 1)"), on the right "Alternatively permitted maximum power of unwanted emissions of a transmitter 1)" ("Alternativ zulässige maximale Leistung unerwünschter Aussendungen eines Senders 1)"). Below them a row with the column numbers "1", "2", "3".
- Row 1: "0.15 MHz – 1.7 MHz" | "60 dB" | the right-hand column block shows "0.25 μW (−36 dBm)".
- Row 2: "1.7 MHz – 35 MHz" | "40 dB" | the right-hand column block still "0.25 μW (−36 dBm)".
- Row 3: "35 MHz – 50 MHz" | "40 dB + 129.1·lg f/35 dB" and below it "with f … frequency in MHz" ("mit f … Frequenz in MHz") | the right-hand column block "0.25 μW (−36 dBm)".
- Row 4: "50 MHz – 1000 MHz" | "60 dB" | the right-hand column block "0.25 μW (−36 dBm)".
- Row 5: ">1000 MHz – 40 GHz" | "50 dB" | "1 μW (−30 dBm)".
- Footnote at the bottom: "1) The higher of the power values resulting in columns 2 and 3 for a frequency range is permitted." ("1) Der jeweils höhere für einen Frequenzbereich resultierende Leistungswert in Spalten 2 und 3 ist zulässig.")
