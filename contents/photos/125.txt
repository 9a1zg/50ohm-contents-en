1) Short description: Side view of a large ship with the lettering "POLARSTERN", with a blue hull and white superstructure.

2) Detailed description: The photo shows a ship in side view with the bow to the left. On the dark blue hull, in the forward part, is the lettering "POLARSTERN". The superstructure is white, with several orange lifeboats on the sides and two conspicuous red crane frame structures at the front and rear. On the superstructure stand masts with numerous antennas, lattice structures and several spherical radomes. A tall funnel in black and orange rises in the middle.
