- Detailed description: On the left stands a white-and-grey Rigol DSA815 (label "Spectrum Analyzer 9 kHz–1.5 GHz") with a colour display and many keys as well as a large rotary knob. On the display a black measurement grid with a yellow curve can be seen; a marker is shown at about 32.5 MHz and reads around −3 dB. At the bottom right of the instrument two coaxial connectors are in use; from there two black coaxial cables lead to a matt black, rectangular metal enclosure with coaxial sockets on both end faces and mounting lugs, which lies at the front on the greenish table surface. Two small, round black caps also lie on the table. In the background a socket outlet, a toggle switch with a lamp and cable ducts on the wall can be seen.
