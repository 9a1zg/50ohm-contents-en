1) Short description: QSL card of the club station with the special event call sign DA200TF on the occasion of the 200th birthday of the poet Theodor Fontane.

2) Detailed description: The photo shows the QSL card of the club station with the special event call sign DA200TF on the occasion of the 200th birthday of the poet Theodor Fontane. In the middle is the call sign "DA200TF" in large red lettering, above it "German Amateur Radio Special Station" and in small white lettering "ITU: 28". To the left of the call sign a relief portrait of Fontane can be seen, below it in white the inscription "DOK: BRA". Along the bottom edge three award certificates of the "Theodor-Fontane-Diplom" are shown side by side, in the versions "Gold", "Silber" (silver) and "Bronze". In the background the card shows a wide meadow with tall green grass, behind it a yellowish field, a row of trees on the horizon and a cloudy blue and white sky.
