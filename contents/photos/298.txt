2. Detailed description: On the left two black rotary knobs with red centres can be seen; around the upper knob "0 … 30V" is marked, around the lower one "0,1A 0,5A 2,5A". Between them is a rectangular rocker switch lit red, labelled "Ein" (on) at the top and "Aus" (off) at the bottom. Above it is a stylised "EA" logo, and beside it "30V" several times. In the lower left area are "EA‑3023" and "Made in W.-Germany". Along the bottom edge several 4 mm sockets and plugs are visible: among them a green, a red and a black plug with cables attached; "0‑30V" is printed next to the sockets. On the right are two analogue meter movements of the "MONACOR" brand, one above the other: the upper one with the scale "0–30" and the symbol "V" (volts), the lower one with the scale "0–3" and the symbol "A" (amperes); both meters bear the marking "Kl. 2.0" (class 2.0). The case looks metallic/silver, the lettering is black, and the controls are mostly black with red accents.
