Short summary: Chart with three coloured, phase-shifted sine curves (u_L blue, i red, p green) and German text annotations on the direction of power flow in an inductance.

Detailed description: On a white background three sinusoidal curves run against time t: a blue curve labelled u_L, a red curve labelled i (the letter i is circled in red) and a green curve labelled p (the letter p is circled in green). The curves are shifted relative to each other. Two red ellipses highlight texts: at the top right "Power is delivered by the voltage source to the reactance" ("Leistung wird von der Spannungsquelle an den Blindwiderstand abgegeben") and at the bottom left "Power flows back from the reactance to the voltage source" ("Leistung fließt vom Blindwiderstand zur Spannungsquelle zurück"). In the middle, in black lettering: "The sum of the powers over time is zero, i.e. no real power is consumed!" ("Die Summe der Leistungen über die Zeit ist gleich Null, d.h. es wird keine Wirkleistung aufgenommen!") At the bottom, as a caption: "Inductance – current too late." ("Induktivität – Strom zu spät.")
