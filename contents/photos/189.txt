Brief summary: A portable digital multimeter with a blue-lit display shows 455.0 kHz as well as 43 % relative humidity and 21.8 °C.

Detailed description: The device in its grey-black rubber protective case lies on a beige carpet. At the top a red LED glows next to the inscription "NCV Sensor"; above it the indications "dBC" and "%RH" can be seen with small ventilation openings. On the display there is a battery symbol on the left, next to it "43%RH" and on the right "21.8°C"; the large digits in the middle show "455.0", flanked by the units "k" and "Hz". Below the display there are buttons labelled "RANGE", "REL", "Hz%" and "HOLD", on the left a red "MODE" button and on the right a button with a lamp symbol. The central rotary switch is on the right-hand side in the position labelled "Hz %"; further scales around it are marked with, among others, "V", "mV", "CAP" and "Lux". At the bottom four sockets can be seen: "10A", "µA mA", "COM" and "V Hz Ω CAP Temp"; a black plug is inserted in "COM", a red plug with a cable in the right-hand socket, next to them safety and measurement category notes such as "CAT III 600V" and "FUSED MAX 400mA". On the front the markings "VOLTCRAFT" and "MT-52" and "Auto Power Off" are also visible.
