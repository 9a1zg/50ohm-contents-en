1) Short summary: Black measuring instrument with an analogue pointer meter, a large rotary dial, an additional rotary switch and several terminals.

2) Detailed description: The instrument has a rectangular black case and lies on a white surface. At the top left is a rectangular analogue meter with a curved scale and the central marking "0"; on the scale face are "Pontavi Wheatstone" and an Ω symbol. Two black screw terminals sit on the top edge; at the top right two small pin-type connections can be seen. To the right of the meter movement is a smaller rotary switch with a scale ring, labelled 0.1 – 1 – 10 – 100 – 1000. Below the meter movement a narrow metal plate with the inscription "X =" and symbols as well as an Ω sign is screwed on. The lower area is dominated by a large, round rotary dial with fine graduations and numbers from 0 to 50 (marked in steps of 5), in whose centre sits a shaped knob that is easy to grip. The case shows slight scratches and signs of use; several screws are visible.
