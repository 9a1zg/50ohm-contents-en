1) Short description: Box-shaped parcel wrapped in brown paper and tied with string, with a badly worn label bearing the inscription "986 Osnabrück 2". Contents: a brick sent as a complaint about the delay in introducing the Amateur Radio Act in 1949.

2) Detailed description: The photo shows a box-shaped parcel wrapped in brown paper and tied with string, with a badly worn label bearing the inscription "986" and below it "Osnabrück 2". The contents are a brick. Bricks of this shape were sent to the Economic Council by German radio amateurs in 1949 as part of the "brick campaign", to complain about the delay in introducing the Amateur Radio Act.
