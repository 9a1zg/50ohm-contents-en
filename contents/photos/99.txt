1) Short description: Tabular overview of the conditions of use of the frequency ranges for the amateur service in Germany (Annex 1 to the Amateur Radio Ordinance (Amateurfunkverordnung, AFuV)).

2) Detailed description: The table shows the frequency ranges for the amateur service in Germany with the maximum power, separately for the three classes A, E and N. The table is taken from Annex 1 to the Amateur Radio Ordinance (AFuV).
