Short summary: Several battery types (button cell LR41, AAA, AA and 9 V block) are arranged side by side on a white background for a size comparison.

Detailed description: At the bottom left lies a small silver button cell with the inscription "LR41 Camelion 0% Hg". Next to it three batteries stand upright: on the left a slim blue-green AAA cell with a silver top and "AAA" printed on it, in the middle a black AA cell with the printed markings "MIGNON", "AA R6 1.5V" and "0% Hg 0% Cd" and a plus sign at the top edge, on the right a blue rectangular 9 V battery with the inscription "AEROCELL MAXIMUM ENERGY E‑BLOCK 6LR61 9V", a large plus and minus sign on the front and the two metal snap contacts at the top. The 9 V battery is the tallest and widest, the AA cell is of medium height, the AAA cell is thinner and somewhat shorter, and the button cell is flat and considerably smaller than the others.
