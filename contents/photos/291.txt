Summary: Close-up of the inside of an electronic unit with a tightly wound red solenoid and numerous wire taps leading to a multi-wafer rotary switch in the metal chassis.

Detailed description: In the lower part of the picture a red cylindrical inductor lies horizontally, closely wound with dark red copper wire. Along the upper side of the inductor many taps are visible: thin, brownish enamelled wires soldered to individual turns; silvery, shiny drops of solder sit at the solder joints, some with dark discolouration around them. The taps run in arcs upwards and forwards to a stacked, circular rotary switch with several insulating wafers and radially arranged solder tags. In the centre of the switch sits a metal shaft with springs and small mechanical parts. To the left and right the grey metal chassis with screws, springs and brackets can be made out; individual white and grey wires lead into the assembly. The background is mostly grey and metallic; below the inductor a light, white surface can be seen. The overall impression is technical, used and functional, with somewhat untidily routed wires.
