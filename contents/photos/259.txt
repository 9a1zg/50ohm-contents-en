Short description: Chart with German labels showing the dissipation factor tan δ of various types of capacitor against frequency, supplemented by explanatory text and coloured highlights.

Detailed description: On the left are notes in large type: "tan δ is large!" ("tan δ ist groß!") (red), "Capacitors with high losses" ("Stark verlustbehaftete Kondensatoren"), "Dissipation factor tan δ as a function of frequency (pronounced: tangent delta)." ("Verlustfaktor tan δ in Abhängigkeit der Frequenz (sprich: tangens delta)."), "tan δ is very small!" ("tan δ ist sehr klein!") (blue), "Qualitatively 'good' capacitors" ("Qualitativ 'gute' Kondensatoren"), and "Ideal (without losses): tan δ = 0" ("Ideal (ohne Verluste): tan δ = 0") (red). Two green arrows point from these texts to the graph on the right. On the right is a log-log chart: y-axis "tan δ" (with scale values including 10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 10⁻², 10⁻¹, 1), x-axis "f" and "Hz" (scale in powers of ten). Several curves are labelled: "Electrolytic capacitor" ("Elektrolyt‑Kondensator") (rising in an arc and falling again, at higher tan δ values), "Paper" ("Papier") (relatively flat near 10⁻²), "Plastic film" ("Kunststofffolie") (flat and low), "Styroflex" (U-shaped), "Ceramic" ("Keramik") (rising at higher frequencies) and "Mica" ("Glimmer") (very low tan δ values). Two dashed vertical lines mark frequency ranges. Coloured markings: an area circled in orange at the top left at tan δ ≈ 1, an area circled in blue at the bottom left at tan δ ≈ 10⁻⁴, and an orange ellipse at the bottom right around the x-axis label "Hz".
