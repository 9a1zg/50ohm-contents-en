1) Short description: Digital handheld multimeter in a red protective sleeve with the display "0.00" and a central rotary selector switch.

2) Detailed description: The handheld multimeter has a grey LCD at the top, which shows "0.00". Above it "DIGITAL MULTIMETER" is embossed. To the left below the display is a small rectangular push button labelled "AUTO POWER OFF", on the right a similar push button "HOLD". In the middle a large, ribbed rotary selector switch dominates, surrounded by a scale with a red segment and white markings. There are the following measuring ranges: resistance (Ω); DC voltage (V⎓); AC voltage (V~); AC current (A~); DC current (A⎓); battery voltage "BATT". To the right below the rotary selector switch the conformity marks "CE", "TÜV Rheinland" and "GS" can be seen. At the bottom there are four sockets, labelled from left to right: "10A", "mA/Ω/BATT", "COM" and "V", with warning symbols and various printed markings between them. The case is black with a red, rubber-like surround.
