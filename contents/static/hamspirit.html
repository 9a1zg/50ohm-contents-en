Radio amateurs are ...

Considerate: We always operate in such a way that we do not knowingly spoil the enjoyment of others, and we take the interests of others into account.

Loyal: We support the community of radio amateurs in local, national and international clubs and associations.

Progressive: We keep learning and keep our skills and stations up to date.

Friendly: We are patient and give tips and advice to beginners when they want it.

Helpful: We offer cooperation and give assistance.

Balanced: We do not neglect our duties towards family, friends, work, school and society.

Ready: Our stations and our skills are ready to serve society and the world community.
