Examination

In Germany, anyone may receive amateur radio transmissions and own amateur radio equipment. Operating the transmitter of an amateur station requires an authorisation to participate in the amateur service with the assignment of a personal call sign. For this reason the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA) holds amateur radio examinations and, if the examination is passed, certifies the required knowledge in the form of an amateur radio certificate or a corresponding harmonised examination certificate. Only then can the corresponding authorisation to participate in the amateur service with a personal call sign be applied for.

First examinations

Recommended procedure:

  1. You prepare for the relevant examination through a DARC face-to-face course, with a mentor, or on your own with the 50ohm.de platform.
  2. You register for the amateur radio examination with the BNetzA using the registration form.
  3. You take the amateur radio examination on the examination date assigned to you. (Details in the next paragraph.)
  4. After passing the examination, you submit an application for authorisation to participate in the amateur service and can choose a personal preferred call sign. You can search for available preferred call signs in the BNetzA's database.
  5. Once a personal call sign has been assigned, nothing more stands in the way of transmitting.

Details of the examination procedure (first examination)

  • Please bring your identity document to the examination.
  • In the examination, the parts Operating knowledge (B), Regulations (V) and Technical knowledge for the class sought must be passed. For Class N, the technical part N must be passed; for Class E, the technical parts N and E; and for Class A, the technical parts N, E and A. Each part comprises 25 questions and must be completed within a maximum of 45 minutes, except that 60 minutes are available for the part “Technical knowledge for Class A”.
  • The BNetzA provides the following aids for the examination:
    1. Annex 1 to the Amateur Radio Ordinance
    2. Call Sign Plan for the amateur service in Germany
    3. Extracts from the IARU band plan for 2 m and 70 cm
    4. Formula sheet
    5. Scrap paper for calculations.
  • The aids are available bundled as a PDF
  • As your own aids, you must bring a pen and a simple scientific calculator or a non-programmable calculator without text memory.
  • The written examination is conducted with single-choice question papers. The correct answers must be entered on an answer sheet.
  • If you fail the technical part for Class E but have passed the technical part for Class N, you receive a Class N examination certificate.
  • If you fail the technical part for Class A but have passed the technical parts for Class N and Class E, you receive a Class E examination certificate.
  • For each correctly answered question, the candidate receives one point. In each part of the examination, the score needed to pass is 19 of the maximum of 25 points.
  • If the required minimum score has not been reached in only one part of the examination, but at least 17 of the 25 possible points have been reached, an oral re-examination is possible.
  • Parts of the examination that have been failed can be retaken at a new examination date within 24 months of the notification of the examination result. After
    this period, the whole examination must be retaken.

Examinations for people with disabilities

People with disabilities can be granted appropriate compensation for disadvantages in the examination by the Federal Network Agency. This compensates for disadvantages caused by the disability without changing the technical requirements: all examination content and required knowledge must still be demonstrated in full. This also applies to voluntary additional examinations (cf. Order 29/2024).

When applying for admission to the examination, a (specialist) medical certificate or comparable evidence must be submitted that shows the nature and extent of the disability in a way that is understandable even without medical training. We recommend that the certificate should, where possible, already name suitable measures to compensate for disadvantages. What matters is not the specific medical diagnosis, but which impairments exist and to what extent they affect the examination.

The responsible office of the Federal Network Agency decides on the nature and extent of the concessions to be granted. Depending on the nature and extent of the disability, the concession granted for the written parts of the examination may also be an individual examination, which may also be conducted orally where appropriate.

Additional examinations

Recommended procedure:

The procedure is similar to that of the first examination. For the additional examination from Class N to E, or from Class N or Class E to Class A, only the relevant question papers E, or E and A, of the technical part of the examination need to be completed.
