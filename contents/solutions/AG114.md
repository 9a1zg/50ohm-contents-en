The traps marked *a* lie further out than the traps marked *b*. They are therefore used for the next lower frequency band.

The inner traps *b* are tuned to the highest band, the $\qty{10}{\meter}$ band. The traps *a* further out must accordingly be tuned to the $\qty{15}{\meter}$ band.

The $\qty{15}{\meter}$ band is at approximately

$\qty{21.2}{\mega\hertz}$.

At this frequency, the traps marked *a* act as rejectors and limit the effective length of the dipole. Finally, for the $\qty{20}{\meter}$ band, the whole antenna, including the outer wire sections, is effective.
