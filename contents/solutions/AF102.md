The transmit power is increased from $\qty{100}{\watt}$ to $\qty{400}{\watt}$, and so quadrupled:

$\frac{\qty{400}{\watt}}{\qty{100}{\watt}} = 4$

Quadrupling the power corresponds to an increase in level of about $\qty{6}{\decibel}$.

Since one S unit also corresponds to $\qty{6}{\decibel}$, the S meter reading rises by *one S unit*.
