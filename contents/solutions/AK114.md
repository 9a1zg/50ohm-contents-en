## Formulas needed

Formula to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the field strength in the far field:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_A \cdot G_i}}{d}$ 

## Data from the question

1. Since it is a dipole antenna: $G_i = \qty{1.64}{}$ 
2. The power at the antenna is: $P_{A} = \qty{10}{\watt}$
3. The distance is: $d = \qty{10}{\meter}$

## Solution

Calculating $E$:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{10}{\watt} \cdot \qty{1.64}{}}}{\qty{10}{\meter}} = \qty{2.2}{\volt\per\meter}$

## Interpretation

The electric field strength determined is $\qty{2.2}{\volt\per\meter}$.
