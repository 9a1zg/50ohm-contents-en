As shown in the circuit, the following applies to the output frequency of the VCO when the PLL is locked:

$f_{VCO} = n \cdot f_A$

Rearranged for the division ratio *n*, this gives:

$n = \frac{f_{VCO}}{f_A}$

For the *lower limit* of the desired output range, with $f_{VCO} = \qty{12.000}{\mega\hertz}$ and $f_A = \qty{12.5}{\kilo\hertz}$, it follows that:

$n_{min} = \frac{\qty{12000000}{\hertz}}{\qty{12500}{\hertz}} = 960$

For the *upper limit*, with $f_{VCO} = \qty{14.000}{\mega\hertz}$, it follows that:

$n_{max} = \frac{\qty{14000000}{\hertz}}{\qty{12500}{\hertz}} = 1120$

The division ratio *n* must therefore vary in the range from *960* to *1120*.
