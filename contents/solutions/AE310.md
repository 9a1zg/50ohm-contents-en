We need $\Delta f_{\textrm{T}}$ for a given bandwidth $B$:

$B\approx2 \cdot \left(\Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}} \right)$

$\qty{12.5}{\kilo\hertz} \approx2 \cdot \left(\Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}} \right)$

$\qty{6.25}{\kilo\hertz} \approx \Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}}$

from which it follows in turn that each of the two terms must be smaller:

$\Delta f_{\textrm{T}} < \qty{6.25}{\kilo\hertz}$

This applies only to the smallest of the four answers: $\qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}$.
