The common-collector circuit has a voltage gain just below $1$, typically about $\numrange{0.9}{0.98}$. Since the output voltage at the emitter follows the input voltage at the base, the two signals are in phase and the phase shift is $\qty{0}{\degree}$.

As a reminder: 

| l: Property | X: Common-emitter circuit | X: Common-collector circuit |
| Phase shift | $\qty{180}{\degree}$ | $\qty{0}{\degree}$ |
| Voltage gain | $\num{100}\dots\num{300}$ | $\num{0.9}\dots\num{0.98}$ |
| Input impedance | high | high |
| Output impedance | high | low |
