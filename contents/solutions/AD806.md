An attenuator with $\qty{20}{\decibel}$ attenuation reduces the power by a factor of $\num{100}$.

With a power of $\qty{100}{\watt}$ fed in, therefore, only

$P_\mathrm{Aus} = \frac{\qty{100}{\watt}}{100} = \qty{1}{\watt}$

reaches the matched load resistor $R_L$.

The rest of the power is converted into heat in the attenuator:

$P_\mathrm{V} = \qty{100}{\watt} - \qty{1}{\watt} = \qty{99}{\watt}$

A total of $\qty{99}{\watt}$ is therefore converted into heat in the attenuator.
