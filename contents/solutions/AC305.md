We are given a match from $\qty{50}{\ohm}$ to $\qty{450}{\ohm}$.

First we determine the impedance ratio required:

$\frac{450}{50} = 9$

The transformer must therefore provide an impedance ratio of $1:9$.

For transformers:
the impedance ratio is the square of the turns ratio:

$\left(\frac{N_2}{N_1}\right)^2 = 9$

From this it follows for the turns ratio:

$\frac{N_2}{N_1} = \sqrt{9} = 3$

So what is required is a turns ratio of:

$3:1$
