The connection in the middle makes the two middle points electrically identical.

As a result:

* $R_1$ and $R_3$ are in parallel between a and the midpoint
* $R_2$ and $R_4$ are in parallel between the midpoint and b

The circuit therefore consists of two parallel connections, which are then in series.

First $R_1$ and $R_3$ are combined:

$ R_{13} = \frac{R_1 \cdot R_3}{R_1 + R_3} $

Since both resistors have the same value:

$ R_{13} = \frac{\qty{2.2}{\kilo\ohm}}{2} = \qty{1.1}{\kilo\ohm} $

Now $R_2$ and $R_4$ are combined:

$ R_{24} = \frac{R_2 \cdot R_4}{R_2 + R_4} $

These resistors also have the same value:

$ R_{24} = \frac{\qty{220}{\ohm}}{2} = \qty{110}{\ohm} $

The two equivalent resistances are in series:

$ R_\mathrm{ges} = R_{13} + R_{24} = \qty{1100}{\ohm} + \qty{110}{\ohm} = \qty{1210}{\ohm} $
