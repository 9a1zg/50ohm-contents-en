The circuit works as a voltage stabiliser with a Zener diode and a transistor. The Zener diode holds the base voltage of the transistor at about $\qty{5.6}{\volt}$.

The emitter voltage of the transistor appears at output A. It is lower than the base voltage by about the base-emitter voltage:

$ U_\mathrm{A} \approx U_\mathrm{Z} - U_\mathrm{BE} \approx \qty{5.6}{\volt} - \qty{0.6}{\volt} \approx \qty{5.0}{\volt} $

The output voltage between A and B is therefore approximately $\qty{5}{\volt}$.
