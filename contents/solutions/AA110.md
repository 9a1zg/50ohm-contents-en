# Given
Levels: $\qty{0}{\dBm}$, $\qty{3}{\dBm}$, $\qty{20}{\dBm}$  

# Solution
We use the $\unit{\dBm}$ formula from the formula sheet (levels, power and voltage levels):  
$ p = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{P}{\qty{1}{\milli\watt}}\right)\unit{\dBm}$  
and solve for $P$:
$P = \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{p}{\qty{10}{\dBm}}}$
We substitute the values from the question and obtain:  

$P = \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{\qty{0}{\dBm}}{\qty{10}{\dBm}}}= \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{0}= \qty{1}{\milli\watt}$

$P = \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{\qty{3}{\dBm}}{\qty{10}{\dBm}}}= \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{0.3}= \qty{1.995}{\milli\watt}$

$P = \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{\qty{20}{\dBm}}{\qty{10}{\dBm}}}= \qty{1}{\milli\watt}\cdot 10^{2}= \qty{100}{\milli\watt}$

# Shortcut
Anyone who has worked a little with logarithms and the table in the examination aids can quickly reach the correct answer by elimination.  
$\qty{0}{\dBm} \rightarrow \qty{1}{\milli\watt}$ (which leaves only two possible answers).  
$\qty{3}{\dB}$ corresponds to doubling. So $\qty{3}{\dBm} \rightarrow  \qty{2}{\milli\watt}$ (which leaves only one answer).
