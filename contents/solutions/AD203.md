We use the formula from the formula sheet:

$f_g = \frac{1}{2 \pi \cdot R \cdot C}$

But be careful: which values actually have to be taken into account here?

If we look more closely at $C_2$, we see that it is not part of the low-pass filter but only serves to decouple interference on the supply voltage of the circuit. So $C_2$ must not be taken into account here.

The high cut-off frequency and very high input impedance of the audio amplifier are negligible and need not be taken into account either. So the only values we substitute are $R_1 = \qty{4.7}{\kilo\ohm}$ and $C_1 = \qty{6.8}{\nano\farad}$.

Substituting the values:

$\begin{split}f_g &= \frac{1}{2 \pi \cdot 4.7 \cdot \qty{10^3}{\ohm} \cdot 6.8 \cdot \qty{10^{-9}}{\farad}}\\ &\approx \qty{4979}{\hertz}\end{split}$
