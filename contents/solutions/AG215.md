The antenna has a gain of $\qty{10}{\decibel}$ over a half-wave dipole. This corresponds to a power ratio of

$10^{\frac{10}{10}} = 10$.

With a transmitter power of $\qty{100}{\watt}$, the ERP in the direction of maximum radiation is therefore

$\qty{100}{\watt} \cdot 10 = \qty{1000}{\watt}$.

The front-to-back ratio is $\qty{20}{\decibel}$. For powers, this corresponds to a factor of

$10^{\frac{20}{10}} = 100$.

In the opposite direction, the ERP is therefore

$\frac{\qty{1000}{\watt}}{100} = \qty{10}{\watt}$.

In the opposite direction to the beam, the antenna radiates an ERP of $\qty{10}{\watt}$.
