The open-circuit voltage of the voltage source is:

$ U_0 = \qty{13.5}{\volt} $

Under load the terminal voltage falls to:

$ U = \qty{12.4}{\volt} $

The voltage dropped across the internal resistance is therefore:

$ U_i = \qty{13.5}{\volt} - \qty{12.4}{\volt} = \qty{1.1}{\volt} $

A current is also given:

$ I = \qty{0.9}{\ampere} $

Ohm's law gives the internal resistance:

$ R_i = \frac{U_i}{I} = \frac{\qty{1.1}{\volt}}{\qty{0.9}{\ampere}} \approx \qty{1.22}{\ohm} $
