By definition, one S unit corresponds to $\qty{6}{\dB}$.  
Signals so strong that they have an S reading greater than S9 are given in the form S9+$\qty{x}{\dB}$.  
Between S9+$\qty{20}{\dB}$ and S8, the receiver input voltage therefore falls by:  
S9+$\qty{20}{\dB} \rightarrow $ S9: $\qty{20}{\dB}$  
S9 $\rightarrow $ S8: $\qty{6}{\dB}$  
a total of $\qty{26}{\dB}$.
