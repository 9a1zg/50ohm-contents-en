When capacitances are connected in parallel, the individual capacitance values are added directly. The self-capacitance of the inductor is also in parallel with the capacitors here and is therefore included.

First, all the values are converted to picofarads:

$ C_1 = \qty{0.1}{\nano\farad} = \qty{100}{\pico\farad} $

$ C_2 = \qty{1.5}{\nano\farad} = \qty{1500}{\pico\farad} $

$ C_3 = \qty{220}{\pico\farad} $

$ C_\mathrm{Spule} = \qty{1}{\pico\farad} $

Now all the capacitances are added:

$ C_\mathrm{ges} = C_1 + C_2 + C_3 + C_\mathrm{Spule} $

$ C_\mathrm{ges} = \qty{100}{\pico\farad} + \qty{1500}{\pico\farad} + \qty{220}{\pico\farad} + \qty{1}{\pico\farad} = \qty{1821}{\pico\farad} $
