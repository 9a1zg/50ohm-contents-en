The battery has a capacity of $\qty{60}{\ampere\hour}$.

The receiver is to be operated until the battery has been discharged to $\qty{10}{\percent}$ remaining capacity. So only $\qty{90}{\percent}$ of the capacity can be used:

$Q = 0{.}9 \cdot \qty{60}{\ampere\hour} = \qty{54}{\ampere\hour}$

The receiver draws a current of $\qty{0.8}{\ampere}$.

The operating time is given by:

$t = \frac{Q}{I} = \frac{\qty{54}{\ampere\hour}}{\qty{0.8}{\ampere}} = \qty{67.5}{\hour}$

$\qty{0.5}{\hour}$ corresponds to $\qty{30}{\minute}$.

The possible operating time is therefore:

$\qty{67}{\hour}$ and $\qty{30}{\minute}$
