We are looking for the sequence of multipliers in which the interference frequency of $\qty{144}{\mega\hertz}$ occurs as the frequency of an intermediate stage.

## Solution 1:

Since the desired output frequency is $\qty{432}{\mega\hertz}$, we divide it by the interference frequency of $\qty{144}{\mega\hertz}$:

$\frac{\qty{432}{\mega\hertz}}{\qty{144}{\mega\hertz}}=3$

This means that the target frequency of $\qty{432}{\mega\hertz}$ is reached by tripling the interference frequency of $\qty{144}{\mega\hertz}$. So the only answer that can be correct is the one that still contains a tripling at the end. The other answers have a doubling at the end, which means that the interference frequency of $\qty{144}{\mega\hertz}$ cannot occur as the frequency of an intermediate stage.

$\text{Fundamental frequency}\cdot 2\cdot 2\cdot 3\cdot 3$
