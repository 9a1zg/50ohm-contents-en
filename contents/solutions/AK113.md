## Formulas needed

Formulas to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the safety distance in the far field:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{E}$ 

or, rearranged,

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{d}$ 

and for the relationship between EIRP and ERP:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_{S} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_d + 2.15 - a}{10}}$

and also:

$g_i = g_d + 2.15$

hence:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_{S} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_i - a}{10}}$


## Data from the question

1. Since it is a Yagi-Uda antenna: $g_i = \qty{12.15}{\dBi}$ 
2. The power is: $P_{S} = \qty{250}{\watt}$
3. The cable loss is: $a = \qty{0}{\dB}$
3. The distance is: $d = \qty{30}{\meter}$

## Solution steps

1. Calculating $P_\mathrm{EIRP}$:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = \qty{250}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{12.15 - 0}{10}} = \qty{4101.47}{\watt}$

2. Calculating $d$:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{4101.47}{\watt}}}{\qty{30}{\meter}} = \qty{11.7}{\volt\per\meter}$


## Interpretation

The electric field strength determined is $\qty{11.7}{\volt\per\meter}$.
