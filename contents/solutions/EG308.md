Simple $\unit{\dB}$ reasoning: the power is halved on the transmission line (with the optimum SWR of 1).
A factor of 2 corresponds to exactly $\qty{3}{\dB}$ (as a power ratio).
You will have remembered this, or you can find it on the formula sheet in the section on levels (*Pegel*). Since the question is about attenuation, the positive value $\qty{3}{\dB}$ is correct and $\qty{-3}{\dB}$ is wrong.
