To determine the voltage across the smoothing capacitor $C_S$, we need to know that the capacitors charge up to the peak voltage $\hat{U}$ of the secondary voltage $U_{\mathrm{sek}}$ of the transformer.

$\hat{U}=U_{\mathrm{eff}}\cdot\sqrt{2}$

We also have to check whether the transformer has a transformation ratio $ü$. In our example the transformer has $ü = \frac{8}{1}$, so we can rearrange the formula $\frac{8}{1} = \frac{U_{\mathrm{prim}}}{U_{\mathrm{sek}}}$ for $U_{\mathrm{sek}}$. This gives the following equation:

$U_{\mathrm{sek}} = \frac{U_{\mathrm{prim}}}{8} = \frac{U_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \sqrt{2}}{8} = \frac{\qty{230}{\volt} \cdot 1.414}{8} = \frac{\qty{325.22}{\volt}}{8} = \qty{40.65}{\volt}$
