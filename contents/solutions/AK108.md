## Formulas needed

Formulas to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the safety distance in the far field:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{E}$ 

and for the relationship between EIRP and ERP:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_\mathrm{ERP} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_i - a}{10}}$

## Data from the question

1. Since it is a dipole: $g_i = \qty{2.15}{\dBi}$ 
2. The cable loss is: $a = \qty{0.5}{\dB}$
3. The power is: $P_\mathrm{ERP} = \qty{300}{\watt}$
4. The limit for the protection of persons is: $E = \qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}$

## Solution steps

1. Calculating $P_\mathrm{EIRP}$:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = \qty{300}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{2.15 - 0.5}{10}} = \qty{438.65}{\watt}$

2. Calculating $d$:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{438.65}{\watt}}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} = \frac{\qty{114.71}{\volt}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} \approx \qty{4.10}{\meter}$


## Interpretation

The required safety distance is $\qty{4.10}{\meter}$.
