The figure shows a periodic signal. It resembles a square-wave signal.  
Now you need to know that the spectrum of a square-wave signal consists of the fundamental frequency and the odd multiples ($\frac{3}{T}$, $\frac{5}{T}$, ...) of the fundamental frequency.  
The spectrum must therefore have more than one spectral component, but must not show any spectral components at even multiples ($\frac{2}{T}$, $\frac{4}{T}$, ...) of the fundamental frequency.
