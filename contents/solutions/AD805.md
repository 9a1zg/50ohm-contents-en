A symmetrical $\qty{50}{\ohm}$ attenuator is designed so that, when it is terminated with $\qty{50}{\ohm}$, it also presents an impedance of $\qty{50}{\ohm}$ at its input.

Since the load resistor,

$R_L = \qty{50}{\ohm}$

is matched, an impedance of

$Z_\mathrm{in} = \qty{50}{\ohm}$

can therefore also be measured between points $a$ and $b$.

The required impedance is therefore $\qty{50}{\ohm}$.
