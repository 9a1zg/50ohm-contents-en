# Given
Output level: $p_\text{Ausgang} = \qty{20}{\dBW}$  

# Solution
We use the $\unit{\dBW}$ formula from the formula sheet (levels, power and voltage levels):  
$ p = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{P}{\qty{1}{\watt}}\right)\unit{\dBW}$  
and solve for $P$:
$P = \qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{p}{\qty{10}{\dBW}}}$
With $p_\text{Ausgang}$ from the question, the output power is:
$P = \qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{p_\text{Ausgang}}{\qty{10}{\dBW}}}= \qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{\qty{20}{\dBW}}{\qty{10}{\dBW}}}= \qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{2} = \qty{10^2}{\watt}$

# Shortcut
Anyone who has worked a little with logarithms and the table in the examination aids knows that $\qty{20}{\dB} \rightarrow 100$, and so $\qty{20}{\dBW} \rightarrow \qty{100}{\watt} = \qty{10^2}{\watt} $.
