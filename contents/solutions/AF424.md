The potentiometer $R_4$ sets the gate bias and thus the operating point of transistor 1.

If the wiper of $R_4$ is turned towards $U_\mathrm{BIAS}$, the gate voltage of transistor 1 rises. This makes the gate-source voltage $U_\mathrm{GS}$ larger and transistor 1 conducts more. Its drain current rises.

The operating point of transistor 2 is set by the separate preset resistor in the lower branch and is not affected by $R_4$. Its drain current therefore remains constant.
