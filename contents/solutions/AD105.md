First we calculate the inductive reactance of the inductor:

$ X_L = 2 \pi f L $

Substituting the values:

$ X_L = 2 \pi \cdot \qty{1}{\mega\hertz} \cdot \qty{100}{\micro\henry} \approx \qty{628}{\ohm} $

Now we calculate the magnitude of the impedance with Pythagoras' theorem:

$ |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} = \sqrt{(\qty{100}{\ohm})^2 + (\qty{628}{\ohm})^2} \approx \qty{636}{\ohm} $
