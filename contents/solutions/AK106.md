## Formulas needed

Formulas to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the safety distance in the far field:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{E}$

and for the relationship between EIRP and ERP:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_\mathrm{ERP} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_i - a}{10}}$


## Data from the question

1. Since it is a half-wave dipole: $g_i = \qty{2.15}{\dBi}$ 
2. The cable loss is negligible: $a = \qty{0.0}{\dB}$
3. The transmit power is 100 W: $P_\mathrm{ERP} = \qty{100}{\watt}$
4. The limit for the protection of persons is: $E = \qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}$
5. The $\qty{10}{\meter}$ band is considered: $\lambda = \qty{10}{\meter}$


## Solution steps

1. Calculating $P_\mathrm{EIRP}$:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = \qty{100}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{2{.}15 - 0{.}0}{10}} = \qty{100}{\watt} \cdot 10^{0{.}215} \approx \qty{164.1}{\watt}$

2. Calculating the safety distance $d$:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{164.1}{\watt}}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} = \frac{\qty{70.2}{\volt}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} \approx \qty{2.50}{\meter}$

3. Checking the far-field condition:

$d > \frac{\lambda}{2\pi} = \frac{\qty{10}{\meter}}{2\pi} \approx \qty{1.59}{\meter}$


## Interpretation

The far-field condition is met for the $\qty{10}{\meter}$ band. The safety distance is $\qty{2.50}{\meter}$.
