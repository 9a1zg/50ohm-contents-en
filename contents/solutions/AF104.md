From S7 to S9 is two S units. Since one S unit corresponds to $\qty{6}{\decibel}$, this first gives:

$2 \cdot \qty{6}{\decibel} = \qty{12}{\decibel}$

In addition, the S meter shows a further $\qty{+8}{\decibel}$ above S9. So the received level has increased by

$\qty{12}{\decibel} + \qty{8}{\decibel} = \qty{20}{\decibel}$

in total.

An increase in power of $\qty{10}{\decibel}$ corresponds to multiplying it by ten. For $\qty{20}{\decibel}$ this therefore gives:

$\frac{P_2}{P_1} = 10^{\frac{20}{10}} = 10^2 = 100$

The radio amateur has therefore increased the transmit power by a factor of $\num{100}$.
