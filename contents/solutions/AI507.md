# Given
* Displayed frequency: $f_\text{Anzeige} = \qty{14100}{\kilo\hertz}$
* Accuracy: $\pm \qty{0.00001}{\percent}$

# Solution
$\qty{1}{\percent}$ corresponds to $1 \cdot {10^{-2}}$;  
$\qty{0.00001}{\percent}$ corresponds to $0.1 \cdot {10^{-6}}$.  

$\Delta f = \qty{14100}{\kilo\hertz} \cdot \qty{0.00001}{\percent} = \qty{14.1 \cdot \cancel{10^6}}{\hertz} \cdot 0.1\cdot \cancel{10^{-6}} = \qty{1.41}{\hertz}$
