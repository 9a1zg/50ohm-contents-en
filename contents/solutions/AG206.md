Current curve *d* has only one current antinode over the whole length of the dipole. It therefore corresponds to the fundamental of the half-wave dipole, which is about $\qty{40}{\meter}$ long.

The operating frequency is therefore approximately

$f = \qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *d* therefore applies when the dipole is fed at $\qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz}$.
