# Solution
We use the voltage ratio formula from the formula sheet (levels, gain):  
$ g = 20 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{U_2}{U_1}\right)\unit{\dB}$  
With the values from the question ($\frac{U_2}{U_1} = 15$) we obtain:

$ g = 20 \cdot \log_{10}\left(15\right)\unit{\dB} = 20 \cdot \qty{1.176}{\dB} = \qty{23.522}{\dB}$
