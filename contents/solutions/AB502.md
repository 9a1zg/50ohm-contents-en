# Given
Voltage: $U = \qty{230}{\volt}$  
Current: $I = \qty{0.63}{\ampere}$  
Duration: $t = \qty{7}{\hour}$  

# Solution
From the formula sheet we need the formula for energy (work):  

$W = P \cdot t$

and power:  
 
$P = U \cdot I$

Substituting one formula into the other:

$\rightarrow W = U \cdot I \cdot t $

Now substituting the given values:  
$\rightarrow W  = \qty{230}{\volt}\cdot \qty{0.63}{\ampere}\cdot\qty{7}{\hour} = \qty{1014.3}{\volt\ampere\hour}  = \qty{1014.3}{\watt\hour} \approx \qty{1.01}{\kilo\watt\hour}$
