We are looking for the total length of a $5/8$-$\lambda$ vertical antenna.

The wavelength at $\qty{14.2}{\mega\hertz}$ is

$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{\qty{300}{\mega\meter\per\second}}{\qty{14.2}{\mega\hertz}} \approx \qty{21.13}{\meter}$.

For a length of $5/8$ of the wavelength, we first get

$l = \frac{5}{8} \cdot \qty{21.13}{\meter} \approx \qty{13.20}{\meter}$.

Now the shortening factor of $\num{0.97}$ must be taken into account:

$l = \qty{13.20}{\meter} \cdot \num{0.97} \approx \qty{12.80}{\meter}$.

The wire must therefore be about $\qty{12.80}{\meter}$ long in total.
