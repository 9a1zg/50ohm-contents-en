Since the oscillator oscillates above the receive frequency, the image frequency is also above the receive frequency. The spacing between the receive frequency and the image frequency is always twice the intermediate frequency:

$f_\mathrm{S} = f_\mathrm{E} + 2 \cdot f_\mathrm{ZF}$

Substituting the given values gives:

$f_\mathrm{S} = \qty{28.5}{\mega\hertz} + 2 \cdot \qty{10.7}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{28.5}{\mega\hertz} + \qty{21.4}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{49.9}{\mega\hertz}$

The image frequency is therefore $\qty{49.9}{\mega\hertz}$.
