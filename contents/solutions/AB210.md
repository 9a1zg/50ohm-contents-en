The figure $\qty{2200}{\milli\ampere\hour}$ describes the quantity of electric charge stored, or the capacity of the battery.

It states how much current the battery can theoretically deliver over a certain time, for example:

$\qty{2200}{\milli\ampere}$ for one hour or  
$\qty{1100}{\milli\ampere}$ for two hours.

The figure therefore denotes the rated capacity of the battery.
