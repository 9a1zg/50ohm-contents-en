A look at the formula sheet is enough: 

$|X_C| = \frac{1}{\omega\cdot C}$

The capacitive reactance $X_C$ depends on the angular frequency $\omega$ and the capacitance $C$. It is inversely proportional to both quantities, which means that the greater the frequency or the capacitance, the smaller the reactance.

In addition, note that the capacitive reactance $X_C$ is a negative quantity, because it causes a phase shift of $\qty{90}{\degree}$ between voltage and current, with the current leading the voltage. $X_C$ is therefore often given with a negative sign to make this phase shift clear.
