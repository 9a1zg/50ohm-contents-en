We are given the input power $P_1=\qty{2.5}{\watt}$ and the output power $P_2=\qty{38}{\watt}$. For the power gain:

$g=10\cdot\log_{10}\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)$

Substituting the values gives:

$g=10\cdot\log_{10}\left(\frac{\qty{38}{\watt}}{\qty{2.5}{\watt}}\right)=10\cdot\log_{10}(15.2)$

$g\approx 10\cdot 1.182=\qty{11.8}{\dB}$

The power amplifier therefore amplifies the input power by a factor of $15.2$. This corresponds to a power gain of $\qty{11.8}{\dB}$.
