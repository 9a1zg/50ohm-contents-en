The open-circuit voltage is:

$ U_0 = \qty{13.5}{\volt} $

Under load the terminal voltage falls to:

$ U = \qty{13}{\volt} $

The voltage difference is dropped across the internal resistance of the voltage source:

$ U_i = \qty{13.5}{\volt} - \qty{13}{\volt} = \qty{0.5}{\volt} $

A current is also given:

$ I = \qty{2}{\ampere} $

Ohm's law gives the internal resistance:

$ R_i = \frac{U_i}{I} = \frac{\qty{0.5}{\volt}}{\qty{2}{\ampere}} = \qty{0.25}{\ohm} $
