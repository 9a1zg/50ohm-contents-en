For the efficiency, the RF power delivered is divided by the DC power drawn. The RF input power of $\qty{1}{\watt}$ is not taken into account in this simplified definition.

$\eta = \frac{P_\mathrm{HF}}{P_\mathrm{DC}} = \frac{\qty{10}{\watt}}{\qty{25}{\watt}} = \num{0.4}$

Expressed as a percentage:

$\eta = \num{0.4} \cdot \qty{100}{\percent} = \qty{40}{\percent}$

The efficiency of the driver stage is therefore $\qty{40}{\percent}$.
