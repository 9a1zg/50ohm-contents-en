The dipole has a fundamental frequency of approximately $\qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *b* has $\num{4}$ current antinodes in all. The operating frequency is therefore four times the fundamental frequency:

$f = 4 \cdot \qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{14}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *b* therefore applies when the dipole is fed at $\qty{14}{\mega\hertz}$.
