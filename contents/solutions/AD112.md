Since all four resistors have the same value, the two potential dividers on the left and right are built the same way.

The operating voltage of $\qty{10}{\volt}$ is therefore divided equally on each side:

$ U_1 = U_2 = \qty{5}{\volt} $

Measuring points A and B are therefore at the same potential. So there is no voltage difference between A and B.

The bridge voltage is therefore:

$ U_\mathrm{AB} = \qty{0}{\volt} $
