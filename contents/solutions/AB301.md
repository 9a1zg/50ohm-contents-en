For sinusoidal AC signals, power is calculated from the RMS values of current and voltage. Since the amplitude of the current, $I_\mathrm{max} = \hat{I} = \qty{0.5}{\ampere}$, is given, we first have to calculate the RMS value of the current with the formula 

$I_\text{eff} = \frac{\hat{I}}{\sqrt{2}}$ 

This gives the following solution: 

$P=I_\text{eff}^2 \cdot R = \left(\frac{\qty{0.5}{\ampere}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 \cdot \qty{20}{\ohm} = \qty{0.125}{\ampere\squared} \cdot \qty{20}{\ohm} = \qty{2.5}{\watt}$
