# Given
Gain: $g = \qty{16}{\dezibel}$  
Input power: $P_1 = \qty{1}{\watt}$

# Solution 1
We use the formula from the formula sheet (levels, gain, power):  
$ g = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)\unit{\dB}$  
and solve for $P_2$:
$P_2 = P_1 \cdot 10^{\frac{g}{\qty{10}{\dB}}}$  
With the numerical values from the examination question:
$P_2 = \qty{1}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{\qty{16}{\dB}}{\qty{10}{\dB}}} = \qty{39.81}{\watt}$

# Solution 2
We use the table in the formula sheet and what we know about the laws of logarithms:  
The logarithm of a product equals the sum of the  
logarithms of the factors:

$\log\left(a\right)+\log\left(b\right) = \log\left(a \cdot b\right)$

With the values from the question and the table, we obtain for the gain factor:

$\qty{16}{\dB} = \qty{6}{\dB} + \qty{10}{\dB} \rightarrow  4 \cdot 10 = 40$

And so:

$P_2 = \qty{1}{\watt} \cdot 40 = \qty{40}{\watt}$
