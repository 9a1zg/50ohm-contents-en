The two diodes form a double peak rectifier. $\qty{15.3}{\volt}$ is measured at the output. In addition, the forward voltages of the two Schottky diodes, $\qty{0.23}{\volt}$ each, must be taken into account:

$U_\mathrm{SS} = \qty{15.3}{\volt} + 2 \cdot \qty{0.23}{\volt} = \qty{15.76}{\volt}$

The peak value of the RF voltage is therefore:

$\hat U = \frac{U_\mathrm{SS}}{2} = \frac{\qty{15.76}{\volt}}{2} = \qty{7.88}{\volt}$

For the power calculation, the RMS value is needed:

$U_\mathrm{eff} = \frac{\hat U}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\qty{7.88}{\volt}}{\sqrt{2}} \approx \qty{5.57}{\volt}$

The $\qty{56}{\ohm}$ and $\qty{470}{\ohm}$ resistors are in parallel and form an approximately $\qty{50}{\ohm}$ termination:

$R = \qty{56}{\ohm} \parallel \qty{470}{\ohm} \approx \qty{50}{\ohm}$

This gives the RF power:

$P = \frac{U_\mathrm{eff}^2}{R} = \frac{(\qty{5.57}{\volt})^2}{\qty{50}{\ohm}} \approx \qty{0.62}{\watt}$

The RF power is therefore about $\qty{600}{\milli\watt}$.
