If $R_2$ loses contact because of a faulty solder joint, the lower part of the base potential divider is missing. The base is then pulled almost up to the positive operating voltage through $R_1$.

As a result, the base-emitter voltage rises sharply, and the transistor is driven fully on. The collector current is then limited essentially only by the collector resistor $R_C$.

In the fully-on state, the collector voltage falls to a very small value, typically about $\qty{0.1}{\volt}$.
