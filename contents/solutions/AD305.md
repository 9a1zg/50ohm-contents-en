In a bridge rectifier the four diodes must be arranged so that the output always has the same polarity, whichever half cycle of the AC voltage is present at the moment.

The positive terminal is where the two cathodes of the diodes are brought together. The negative terminal is where the two anodes of the diodes are brought together. The two AC connections are on the left and right; the positive terminal is at the top and the negative terminal at the bottom.
