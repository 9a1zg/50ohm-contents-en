The reactance of an ideal inductor is called the inductive reactance and has a positive sign. It depends both on the inductance $L$ of the inductor and on the frequency $f$ of the applied AC voltage.

For the inductive reactance (see the formula sheet):

$X_L = 2\pi f L$

From this we can see:
- The greater the frequency $f$, the greater the reactance.
- The greater the inductance $L$, the greater the reactance too.

The inductive reactance is therefore positive and dependent on the inductance and the frequency.
