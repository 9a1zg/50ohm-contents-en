If $R_1$ loses contact because of a faulty solder joint, the base is no longer supplied with a positive bias through the potential divider.

Through $R_2$, the base is then practically at chassis potential. As a result, the base-emitter voltage is too small, no base current flows and the transistor is off.

So no collector current flows any more either. No voltage is dropped across $R_C$, so the collector voltage rises to the operating voltage.
