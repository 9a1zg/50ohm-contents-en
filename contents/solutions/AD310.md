A full-wave rectifier uses both the positive and the negative half cycle of the AC voltage. This produces two voltage pulses at the output in each period of the mains.

The output frequency is therefore doubled:

$ f_{\mathrm{out}} = 2 \cdot f_{\mathrm{in}} $

With a mains frequency of

$ f_{\mathrm{in}} = \qty{50}{\hertz} $

this gives:

$ f_{\mathrm{out}} = 2 \cdot \qty{50}{\hertz} = \qty{100}{\hertz} $

The fundamental frequency of the output voltage is therefore $\qty{100}{\hertz}$.
