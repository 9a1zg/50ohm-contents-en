The front-to-back ratio states how much more strongly the antenna radiates in the direction of maximum radiation than in the opposite direction.

In direction *b* the ERP is $\qty{15}{\watt}$, in direction *a* only $\qty{0.6}{\watt}$. The power ratio is therefore

$\frac{\qty{15}{\watt}}{\qty{0.6}{\watt}} = 25$.

Since powers are being compared here, the ratio in decibels is calculated with

$10 \cdot \log_{10}(25) \approx \qty{14}{\decibel}$.

The front-to-back ratio is therefore about $\qty{14}{\decibel}$.
