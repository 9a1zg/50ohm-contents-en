A photovoltaic module consists of four strings connected in parallel. Each string contains 30 solar cells.

In a series connection the voltages add:

$ U = 30 \cdot \qty{0.6}{\volt} = \qty{18}{\volt} $

In a series connection the short-circuit current initially stays at:

$ I_\mathrm{SC} = \qty{1}{\ampere} $

Since four such strings are connected in parallel, the currents add:

$ I_\mathrm{ges} = 4 \cdot \qty{1}{\ampere} = \qty{4}{\ampere} $

So the module delivers an open-circuit voltage of $\qty{18}{\volt}$ and a short-circuit current of $\qty{4}{\ampere}$.
