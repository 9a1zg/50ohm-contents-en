For the $\qty{80}{\meter}$ band, the traps are to act as rejectors. Their resonant frequency must therefore lie roughly in this band, that is, at about

$\qty{3.65}{\mega\hertz}$.

At this frequency the traps largely block off the outer part of the antenna. Between the two traps, therefore, there must be approximately a half-wave dipole for the $\qty{80}{\meter}$ band:

$l \approx \frac{\lambda}{2} = \frac{\qty{80}{\meter}}{2} = \qty{40}{\meter}$

So the traps must be resonant at about $\qty{3.65}{\mega\hertz}$, and the distance $l$ between them is about $\qty{40}{\meter}$.
