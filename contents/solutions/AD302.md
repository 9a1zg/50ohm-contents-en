The circuit consists of a transformer, a diode and a smoothing capacitor. The transformer delivers an AC voltage of $\qty{15}{\volt}$ RMS.

The capacitor charges up through the diode to the peak value of the AC voltage. To find it, the RMS value has to be converted into the peak value:

$ \hat{U} = U_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \sqrt{2} $

This gives:

$ \hat{U} = \qty{15}{\volt} \cdot \sqrt{2} \approx \qty{21.2}{\volt} $

The forward voltage of the diode is neglected here as an approximation. So the capacitor holds approximately the peak voltage of the AC voltage.

The open-circuit voltage between A and B is therefore approximately:

$ U_{AB} \approx \qty{21}{\volt} $
