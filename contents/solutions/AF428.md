The total gain of the transmit path is the difference between output and input power in dBm.

At the input of the amplifier chain there is

$P_\mathrm{in}=\qty{-5}{\decibelmilliwatt}$

and at the output

$P_\mathrm{out}=\qty{43}{\decibelmilliwatt}$.

The total gain is therefore:

$G=P_\mathrm{out}-P_\mathrm{in}=\qty{43}{\decibelmilliwatt}-\left(\qty{-5}{\decibelmilliwatt}\right)=\qty{48}{\decibel}$

The total gain of the transmit path is therefore $\qty{48}{\decibel}$.
