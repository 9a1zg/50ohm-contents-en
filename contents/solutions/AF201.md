In a single-conversion superhet, the wanted receive frequency and the image frequency lie on opposite sides of the oscillator frequency. Both are the same distance, $f_\text{ZF}$, from the oscillator frequency (see Figure [ref:spiegelfrequenzen_fe1_fe2]).

The spacing between the receive frequency and the image frequency is therefore:

$\Delta f = 2 \cdot f_\text{ZF}$

This formula can also be found in the formula sheet.

The frequency difference between the receive frequency and the image frequency is therefore always *twice the intermediate frequency (IF)*.
