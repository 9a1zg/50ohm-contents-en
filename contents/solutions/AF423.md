$R_1$ and $R_2$ set the gate bias, and thus the operating point, of each of the two LDMOS transistors separately.

If the wipers of $R_1$ and $R_2$ are each turned towards $U_\mathrm{BIAS}$, the gate voltage of the transistor concerned rises. This makes the gate-source voltage $U_\mathrm{GS}$ larger and the transistors conduct more.

To increase the quiescent current of the output stage, both $R_1$ and $R_2$ must therefore be turned towards $U_\mathrm{BIAS}$.
