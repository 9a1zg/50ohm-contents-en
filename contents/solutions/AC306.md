We are given a match from $\qty{50}{\ohm}$ to $\qty{2500}{\ohm}$.

First we determine the impedance ratio required (seen from the antenna):

$ \frac{Z_P}{Z_S} = \frac{2500}{50} = 50$

The transformer must therefore provide an impedance ratio of about $1:50$.

For transformers:
the impedance ratio is the square of the turns ratio:

$\left(\frac{N_2}{N_1}\right)^2 = 50$

From this it follows for the turns ratio:

$\frac{N_2}{N_1} = \sqrt{50} \approx 7$

So what is required is a turns ratio of approximately:

$1:7$
