For the meter in the bridge diagonal to show no voltage, the bridge must be balanced, i.e. the voltage at point A must be equal to the voltage at point B. 

This means that the (unloaded) divider ratios on the left and right must be equal:

$ \frac{R_1}{R_2} = \frac{R_3}{R_4} $

Measuring points A and B are then at the same potential, and there is no voltage between them.
