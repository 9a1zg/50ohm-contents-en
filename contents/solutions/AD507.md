An RF signal at $\qty{3.7}{\mega\hertz}$ and an AF signal at $\qty{1}{\kilo\hertz}$ are fed to the diode. Because of the non-linear characteristic of the diode, the amplitude of the RF signal varies in step with the AF signal.

The tuned circuit that follows is tuned to the carrier frequency of $\qty{3.7}{\mega\hertz}$ and suppresses unwanted frequency components.

The circuit therefore produces an amplitude-modulated signal and is an AM modulator.
