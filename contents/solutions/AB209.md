The circuit consists of six cells of $\qty{2}{\volt}$ each, connected in series.

In a series connection the voltages add:

$U_\mathrm{ges} = 6 \cdot \qty{2}{\volt} = \qty{12}{\volt}$

The capacity in ampere-hours stays the same in a series connection. Each cell has a capacity of $\qty{10}{\ampere\hour}$, so the whole battery also has

$\qty{10}{\ampere\hour}$.

So the battery has the ratings:

$\qty{12}{\volt} / \qty{10}{\ampere\hour}$
