In the circuit shown, the P-region is at $\qty{0}{\volt}$ and the N-region at $\qty{+5}{\volt}$. The diode is therefore reverse-biased.

With a reverse voltage, the free charge carriers are pulled away from the junction. As a result, the depletion region, or depletion layer, grows.
