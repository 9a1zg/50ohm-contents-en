From S9 to S8 the reading drops by one S unit. One S unit corresponds to $\qty{6}{\decibel}$.

A reduction of $\qty{6}{\decibel}$ corresponds to halving the voltage:

$U_2 = \frac{U_1}{2} = \frac{\qty{50}{\micro\volt}}{2} = \qty{25}{\micro\volt}$

The receiver input voltage therefore drops to $\qty{25}{\micro\volt}$.
