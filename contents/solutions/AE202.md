The carrier amplitude 

$\hat{U}_{\mathrm{T}}$

and the amplitude of the modulation 

$\hat{U}_{\mathrm{mod}}$

can be read from the oscillogram. One square corresponds to $\qty{3}{\volt}$.

Here is the sketch from the formula sheet: 

[picture:328:modulationsgrad:Modulation depth of an AM signal]

The unmodulated carrier amplitude is two squares:

$\hat{U}_\mathrm{T} = 2 \cdot \qty{3}{\volt} = \qty{6}{\volt}$

The modulation changes the envelope by one square upwards and downwards:

$\hat{U}_\mathrm{mod} = 1 \cdot \qty{3}{\volt} = \qty{3}{\volt}$

The formula for the modulation depth gives:

$m = \frac{\hat{U}_\mathrm{mod}}{\hat{U}_\mathrm{T}} = \frac{\qty{3}{\volt}}{\qty{6}{\volt}} = \num{0.5}$

The modulation depth is therefore $\qty{50}{\percent}$.
