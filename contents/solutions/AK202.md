## Explanation

Low-resistance equipotential bonding serves the *protection of persons* by bringing all conductive parts that can be touched at the same time to almost the same electric potential.

This prevents dangerous touch voltages between cases, equipment and metal parts of the installation, or reduces them to a non-critical level.

In the event of a fault or of potential differences, an equalising current is carried through the equipotential bonding conductor, so that no voltage differences, or only very small ones, appear on metal parts that can be touched.

This prevents a dangerous current from flowing through a person's body when different conductive parts are touched at the same time.
