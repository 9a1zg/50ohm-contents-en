When calculating $X_C$, the powers of ten must always be taken into account. The formula is:

$X_C = \frac{1}{\omega \cdot C} = \frac{1}{{2\pi \cdot f \cdot C}}$

The following values must be substituted: $C = \qty{10}{\pico\farad} = \qty{10e-12}{\farad}$ and $f = \qty{100}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{100e6}{\hertz}$

A step-by-step calculation with these values could look like this, if for simplicity we use the value $\num{6.28}$ for $2\pi$:

$\begin{split} 6.28 \cdot 10 \cdot 100 &= 6280 \\ 10^{-12} \cdot 10^6 &= 10^{-6} \\ \num{6280} \cdot 10^{-6} &= \num{0.00628} \\ 1/\num{0.00628} &= \num{159} \end{split}$
