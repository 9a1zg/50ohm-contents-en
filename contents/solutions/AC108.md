In question AC108, the capacitance has to be calculated from the capacitive reactance $X_C$ and the frequency:
* First $X_C$ is calculated:
  $X_C = \frac{U}{I} = \frac{\qty{16}{\volt}}{\qty{32}{\milli\ampere}} = \qty{500}{\ohm}$
* Then the formula for $X_C$ from the formula sheet is rearranged for $C$ and the values are substituted:
  $C = \frac{1}{2\pi \cdot f \cdot X_C} = \frac{1}{6.28 \cdot \qty{50}{\hertz} \cdot \qty{500}{\ohm}} \approx 6.37\cdot \qty{10^{-6}}{\farad} = \qty{6.37}{\micro\farad}$
* The power of ten $10^{-6}$ appears in the result and has to be converted into a prefix.
