The question could also be put as "What are typical bandwidths in amateur radio?". The answer is in the IARU band plans among the examination aids. On pages 9 and 10, the column "Max." gives the following values: 
* $\qty{500}{\hertz}$
* $\qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz}$
* $\qty{12}{\kilo\hertz}$
* $\qty{20}{\kilo\hertz}$

The only one of the four answers that consists solely of these values is the correct one: "SSB: $\qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz}$; RTTY: $\qty{500}{\hertz}$; FM: $\qty{12}{\kilo\hertz}$".
