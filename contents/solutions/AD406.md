With the transistor cut off, the output signal is at first at about $\qty{10}{\volt}$. When the positive half cycle of the input voltage exceeds the base-emitter threshold voltage of approximately $\qty{0.6}{\volt}$, the transistor starts to conduct. As a result, the collector current rises, a larger voltage is dropped across the collector resistor and the output voltage $U_A$ falls. During the negative half cycle the transistor cuts off again and $U_A$ rises back to about $\qty{10}{\volt}$. Downward-pointing pulses are therefore produced during the positive half cycles of the input.
