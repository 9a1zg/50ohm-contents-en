First, the intermediate frequency is determined from the wanted receive frequency and the oscillator frequency:

$f_\mathrm{ZF} = \left|f_\mathrm{E}-f_\mathrm{OSZ}\right| = \left|\qty{28.3}{\mega\hertz}-\qty{39}{\mega\hertz}\right| = \qty{10.7}{\mega\hertz}$

Since the oscillator frequency is above the wanted receive frequency, the image frequency is also above the oscillator frequency. It is the same distance, $f_\mathrm{ZF}$, from the oscillator frequency:

$f_\mathrm{S} = f_\mathrm{OSZ}+f_\mathrm{ZF} = \qty{39}{\mega\hertz}+\qty{10.7}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{49.7}{\mega\hertz}$

The image frequency is therefore $\qty{49.7}{\mega\hertz}$.
