We are looking for the total length of the wire of a full-wave delta loop antenna.

The wavelength at $\qty{7.1}{\mega\hertz}$ is

$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{\qty{300}{\mega\meter\per\second}}{\qty{7.1}{\mega\hertz}} \approx \qty{42.25}{\meter}$.

For a full-wave loop antenna, the correction factor of $\num{1.02}$ must also be taken into account:

$l = \qty{42.25}{\meter} \cdot \num{1.02} \approx \qty{43.10}{\meter}$.

The wire must therefore be about $\qty{43.10}{\meter}$ long in total.
