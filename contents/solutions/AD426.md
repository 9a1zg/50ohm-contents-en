We are given the power gain $g=\qty{16}{\dB}$ and the input power $P_1=\qty{1}{\watt}$. For the power ratio:

$g=10\cdot\log_{10}\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)$

Rearranged for the output power $P_2$:

$P_2=P_1\cdot 10^{\frac{g}{10}}$

Substituting the values gives:

$P_2=\qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{\frac{16}{10}}=\qty{1}{\watt}\cdot 10^{1.6}\approx\qty{39.8}{\watt}$

The RF output power to be expected is therefore approximately $\qty{40}{\watt}$.
