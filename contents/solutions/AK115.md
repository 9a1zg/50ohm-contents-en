## Formulas needed

Formula to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the field strength in the far field:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{d}$ 

and for the relationship between $P_\mathrm{EIRP}$ and $P_\mathrm{ERP}$:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_\mathrm{ERP} \cdot 1.64$

hence:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{ERP} \cdot 1.64}}{d}$ 


## Data from the question

1. The effective radiated power is: $P_\mathrm{ERP} = \qty{100}{\watt}$
2. The distance is: $d = \qty{100}{\meter}$

## Solution

Calculating $E$:

$E = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{100}{\watt} \cdot \qty{1.64}{}}}{\qty{100}{\meter}} = \qty{0.7}{\volt\per\meter}$

## Interpretation

The electric field strength determined is $\qty{0.7}{\volt\per\meter}$.
