The circuit diagram shows an oscillator circuit whose frequency of
oscillation is given by the resonant frequency of a parallel tuned circuit.

The inductive component of this tuned circuit is the inductor;
the capacitive component of the tuned circuit is made up of several
parts:

* the capacitors next to the inductor
* the varactor diode

The capacitance of the capacitors is constant, but the capacitance
of the varactor diode depends on the voltage applied
across it. If an audio signal is fed in through the input resistor
on the far left, the voltage across the varactor diode
varies with the amplitude of the audio signal. As the voltage
changes, the capacitance of the varactor diode changes; as the capacitance
changes, the resonant frequency of the tuned circuit changes,
and with it the output frequency of the oscillator, i.e. a
change in the amplitude of the input signal becomes a change in the frequency of the
output signal, and that is *frequency modulation*.

For this reason, the circuit shows a modulator for generating frequency-modulated signals.
