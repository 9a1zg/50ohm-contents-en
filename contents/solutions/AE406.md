Two bits are transmitted per symbol. The data rate is therefore the symbol rate multiplied by the number of bits per symbol:

$R_\mathrm{Daten} = R_\mathrm{Symbol} \cdot \qty{2}{\bit\per{symbol}}$

With a symbol rate of $\qty{23.4}{\baud}$, this gives:

$R_\mathrm{Daten} = \qty{23.4}{\baud} \cdot 2 = \qty{46.8}{\bit\per\second}$

The data rate is therefore $\qty{46.8}{\bit\per\second}$.
