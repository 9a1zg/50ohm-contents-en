The power fed in is $\qty{100}{\watt}$. On its way to the end of the line, it is reduced to half by the line loss of $\qty{3}{\decibel}$:

$P_\mathrm{Ende}=\qty{100}{\watt}\cdot 10^{-\frac{3}{10}}\approx\qty{50}{\watt}$.

With an open circuit or a short circuit, the power arriving at the end of the line is completely reflected. So approximately

$\qty{50}{\watt}$

is reflected there.

On the way back to the transmitter, the reflected power is attenuated by the line by another $\qty{3}{\decibel}$. So only about $\qty{25}{\watt}$ would come back at the line input.

The question, however, asks for the power reflected at the end of the line. This is about $\qty{50}{\watt}$.
