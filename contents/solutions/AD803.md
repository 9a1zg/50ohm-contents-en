An attenuator with $\qty{20}{\decibel}$ attenuation reduces the power by a factor of $\num{100}$.

The power ratio is given by:

$\qty{20}{\decibel} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_\mathrm{IN}}{P_\mathrm{RL}}\right)$

It follows that:

$\frac{P_\mathrm{IN}}{P_\mathrm{RL}} = 10^{\frac{20}{10}} = 10^2 = 100$

The ratio between the input power and the power in the load resistor is therefore $\num{100}$.
