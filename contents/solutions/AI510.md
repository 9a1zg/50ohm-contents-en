# Given
* Beacon frequency: $f_\text{Baken} = \qty{144.4}{\mega\hertz}$
* SSB bandwidth: $f_{B,max} = \qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz}$
* Accuracy: $\qty{1}{\ppm}$

# Preliminary consideration
The spacing ($f_\text{Abstand}$) from the beacon frequency ($f_\text{Baken}$) is made up of the SSB bandwidth ($f_{B,max}$) and the (safety) margin ($\Delta f$) due to the inaccuracy of $\qty{1}{\ppm}$. $\unit{ppm}$ stands for *parts per million*, so $\qty{1}{\ppm}$ means $1$ per $10^6$. 

# Solution
$\Delta f = \qty{144.4}{\mega\hertz} \cdot \frac{1}{10^6} = \qty{144.4 \cdot \cancel{10^6}}{\hertz}\frac{1}{\cancel{10^6}} = \qty{144.4}{\hertz}= \qty{0.1444}{\kilo\hertz}$

$f_\text{Abstand} = f_{B,max} + \Delta f = \qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz} + \qty{0.1444}{\kilo\hertz} = \qty{2.8444}{\kilo\hertz}$
