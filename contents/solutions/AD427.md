Since the input and output resistances are equal, the voltage gain can be calculated directly from the ratio of output to input voltage:

$g = 20 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{U_\mathrm{A}}{U_\mathrm{E}}\right)$

Substituting the given voltages:

$g = 20 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{\qty{4}{\milli\volt}}{\qty{1}{\milli\volt}}\right) = 20 \cdot \log_{10}(4)$

$g \approx 20 \cdot \num{0.602} \approx \qty{12}{\dB}$

The voltage gain of the amplifier is therefore approximately $\qty{12}{\dB}$.
