The transformation ratio is $20:1$. From the mains voltage of $\qty{230}{\volt}$ this gives a secondary voltage of

$ U_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{\qty{230}{\volt}}{20} = \qty{11.5}{\volt} $

The capacitor charges up to the peak value of the AC voltage:

$ \hat{U} = U_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \sqrt{2} = \qty{11.5}{\volt} \cdot \sqrt{2} \approx \qty{16.3}{\volt}
$

In addition, a safety margin of $\qty{50}{\percent}$ is to be allowed for:

$ U_{\mathrm{C}} = \qty{16.3}{\volt} \cdot 1{.}5 \approx \qty{24.5}{\volt} $

The capacitor should therefore have a voltage rating of at least $\qty{25}{\volt}$.
