We are looking for the length of a coaxial cable for one whole wavelength at $\qty{145}{\mega\hertz}$.

The wavelength in free space is

$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{\qty{300}{\mega\meter\per\second}}{\qty{145}{\mega\hertz}} \approx \qty{2.07}{\meter}$.

The signal travels more slowly in the coaxial cable. With the velocity factor of $\num{0.66}$, we therefore get

$l = \qty{2.07}{\meter} \cdot \num{0.66} \approx \qty{1.37}{\meter}$.

The coaxial cable must therefore be about $\qty{1.37}{\meter}$ long.
