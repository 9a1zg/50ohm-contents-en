The transformation ratio is $5:1$. From the mains voltage of $\qty{230}{\volt}$ this gives a secondary voltage of

$ U_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{\qty{230}{\volt}}{5} = \qty{46}{\volt} $

What determines the voltage rating of the diode is twice the maximum peak voltage. This is calculated as:

$ U_{\mathrm{Sperr}} = 2 \cdot \hat{U} = 2\cdot U_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \sqrt{2} = 2 \cdot \qty{46}{\volt} \cdot \sqrt{2} \approx \qty{130}{\volt} $

In addition, a safety margin of $\qty{20}{\percent}$ is to be allowed for:

$ U_{\mathrm{D}} = \qty{130}{\volt} \cdot 1{.}2 \approx \qty{156}{\volt} $

The voltage rating of the diode should therefore be at least $\qty{156}{\volt}$.
