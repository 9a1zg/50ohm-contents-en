The traps marked *b* lie closer to the feed point than the traps marked *a*. At their resonant frequency they therefore limit the effective dipole to the shortest section of the antenna.

The shortest dipole is needed for the highest of the three frequency bands. Of the $\qty{20}{\meter}$, $\qty{15}{\meter}$ and $\qty{10}{\meter}$ bands, the $\qty{10}{\meter}$ band has the highest frequency.

The traps marked *b* must therefore be tuned approximately to the $\qty{10}{\meter}$ band, that is, to

$\qty{29.0}{\mega\hertz}$.
