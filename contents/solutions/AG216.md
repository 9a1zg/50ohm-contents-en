The antenna has a gain of $\qty{15}{\decibel}$ over the half-wave dipole in the direction of maximum radiation. The front-to-back ratio is $\qty{25}{\decibel}$. In the backward direction the gain is therefore $\qty{25}{\decibel}$ lower:

$\qty{15}{\decibel} - \qty{25}{\decibel} = \qty{-10}{\decibel}$.

A power difference of $\qty{-10}{\decibel}$ corresponds to the factor

$10^{\frac{-10}{10}} = 0{.}1$.

With a transmitter power of $\qty{6}{\watt}$, this gives an ERP in the opposite direction to the beam of

$\qty{6}{\watt} \cdot 0{.}1 = \qty{0.6}{\watt}$.

In the opposite direction to the beam, the antenna radiates an ERP of $\qty{0.6}{\watt}$.
