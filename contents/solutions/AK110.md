## Formulas needed

Formulas to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the safety distance in the far field:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_\mathrm{EIRP}}}{E}$ 

and for the relationship between EIRP and ERP:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = P_\mathrm{ERP} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_d + 2{.}15 - a}{10}}$

## Data from the question

1. Since it is a Yagi-Uda antenna: $g_d = \qty{11.5}{\dBd}$ 
2. The cable loss is: $a = \qty{1.5}{\dB}$
3. The power is: $P_\mathrm{ERP} = \qty{75}{\watt}$
4. The limit for the protection of persons is: $E = \qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}$

## Solution steps

1. Calculating $P_\mathrm{EIRP}$:

$P_\mathrm{EIRP} = \qty{75}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{11.5 + 2.15 - 1.5}{10}} = \qty{1230.44}{\watt}$

2. Calculating the safety distance $d$:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{1230.44}{\watt}}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} = \frac{\qty{192.12}{\volt}}{\qty{28}{\volt\per\meter}} = \qty{6.86}{\meter}$


## Interpretation

The safety distance determined is $\qty{6.86}{\meter}$.
