The odd harmonics have the ordinal numbers $1$, $3$, $5$, $\ldots$

The second odd harmonic is therefore the 3rd harmonic:

$f_3 = 3 \cdot f_0$

With $f_0 = \qty{144.690}{\mega\hertz}$ this gives:

$f_3 = 3 \cdot \qty{144.690}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{434.070}{\mega\hertz}$

The second odd harmonic is therefore at $\qty{434.070}{\mega\hertz}$.
