An attenuator with $\qty{6}{\decibel}$ attenuation reduces the power by a factor of approximately $\num{4}$.

The power ratio is given by:

$\qty{6}{\decibel} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_\mathrm{IN}}{P_\mathrm{RL}}\right)$

It follows that:

$\frac{P_\mathrm{IN}}{P_\mathrm{RL}} = 10^{\frac{6}{10}} \approx \num{3.98} \approx \num{4}$

The ratio between the input power and the power in the load resistor is therefore approximately $\num{4}$.
