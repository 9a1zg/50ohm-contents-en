For USB, the upper sideband must fall into the passband of the $\qty{9}{\mega\hertz}$ crystal filter.

[picture:941:a_balancemodulator_usb:Balanced modulator with suppressed carrier and crystal filter for USB]
[picture:940:a_balancemodulator_lsb:Balanced modulator with suppressed carrier and crystal filter for LSB]

The crystal filter is about $\qty{3}{\kilo\hertz}$ wide. Its filter edges therefore lie about $\qty{1.5}{\kilo\hertz}$ below and above the centre frequency of $\qty{9}{\mega\hertz}$.

For LSB, the frequency of the suppressed carrier is placed at the upper filter edge. The lower sideband then lies within the passband of the filter.

For USB, the frequency of the suppressed carrier is placed at the lower filter edge. The upper sideband then lies within the passband of the filter:

$f_\mathrm{OSZ} = \qty{9}{\mega\hertz} - \qty{1.5}{\kilo\hertz} = \qty{8.9985}{\mega\hertz}$

The AF is therefore mixed with an oscillator frequency of $\qty{8.9985}{\mega\hertz}$.
