First we have to determine the resonant frequency of the tuned circuit from the values of *L* and *C* using Thomson's formula, since the equations given above apply only at resonance!

$f = \frac{1}{2 \pi \cdot \sqrt{L \cdot C}}$

Substituting the values:

$\begin{split} f &= \frac{1}{2 \pi \cdot \sqrt{100 \cdot \qty{10^{-6}}{\henry} \cdot 0.01 \cdot 10^{-6}\text{ F}}} \\ &\approx \qty{159154.94}{\hertz} \approx 159.2 \cdot \qty{10^3}{\hertz}\\ &\approx \qty{159.2}{\kilo\hertz}\end{split}$
  
With this, the inductive reactance $X_\text{L}$ can be calculated as follows (formulas from the formula sheet):

Angular frequency: $\omega = 2 \pi \cdot f$

Inductive reactance: $X_\text{L} = \omega \cdot L$

Substituting the values:
$\begin{split}X_\text{L} &= 2 \pi \cdot 159.2 \cdot \qty{10^3}{\hertz} \cdot 100 \cdot \qty{10^{-6}}{\henry}\\ &\approx \qty{100.03}{\ohm}\end{split}$
  
Taking the ohmic resistance $R_\text{S}$ into account, the Q factor is then:
  
$\begin{split}Q &= \frac{X_\text{L}}{R_\text{S}}\\\text{Substituting the values:}\\Q &= \frac{\qty{100.03}{\ohm}}{\qty{10}{\ohm}} \approx 10\end{split}$
