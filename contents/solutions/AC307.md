To calculate with the current density, the cross-sectional area in $\unit{\milli\meter\squared}$ must be found from the wire diameter.
Cross-sectional area (see the formula sheet – keyword: resistance of wires (*Widerstand von Drähten*)):

$A_\mathrm{Dr} = d^2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4}$

Cross-sectional area of the wire: $A_\mathrm{Dr} = (\qty{0.5}{\milli\meter})^2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4} = \qty{0.196}{\milli\meter\squared}$

The question can only be solved with the formula for the "current rating of windings" (*Belastbarkeit von Wicklungen*):

$I = S \cdot A_\mathrm{Dr} = \qty{2.5}{\ampere\per\milli\meter\squared} \cdot \qty{0.196}{\milli\meter\squared} \approx \qty{0.49}{\ampere}$
