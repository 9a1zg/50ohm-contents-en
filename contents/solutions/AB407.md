The figure shows a frequency spectrum with spectral components at odd multiples ($\frac{3}{T}$, $\frac{5}{T}$, ...) of the fundamental frequency. This spectrum belongs to a square-wave signal.  
# By elimination
The other answer options show sinusoidal signals. These have no multiples of the fundamental frequency in their spectrum.  
(Varying the amplitude and the phase has no effect on the position of the spectral components. Varying the mean value of the signal only produces a spectral component at $f=\qty{0}{\hertz}$.)
