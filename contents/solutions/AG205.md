The dipole has a fundamental frequency of approximately $\qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *c* has $\num{2}$ current antinodes in all. The operating frequency is therefore twice the fundamental frequency:

$f = 2 \cdot \qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{7}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *c* therefore applies when the dipole is fed at $\qty{7}{\mega\hertz}$.
