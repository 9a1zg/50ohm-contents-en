Given: 

* Speech signal with a bandwidth of $f_\text{max}=\qty{4}{\kilo\hertz}$

Required: 

* Sampling rate $f_\text{s}$

Solution:

For error-free sampling to be possible, the Nyquist condition must be satisfied: 

 $f_\text{s} > 2 \cdot f_\text{max}$

Substituting the values: 

$\begin{split} f_\text{s} &> 2 \cdot f_\text{max}\\ &> 2 \cdot \qty{4000}{\hertz}\\ &> \qty{8000}{\hertz} \end{split}$

This leaves only the answer $\qty{9600}{\sps}$, since only this value is greater than $\qty{8000}{\hertz}$.
