The load resistance is $\qty{150}{\ohm}$, while the meter and the transmitter are designed for a characteristic impedance of $\qty{50}{\ohm}$.

Since the load resistance is greater than the characteristic impedance, the standing wave ratio is

$s=\frac{R_\mathrm{L}}{Z}$.

This gives

$s=\frac{\qty{150}{\ohm}}{\qty{50}{\ohm}}=3$.

The SWR meter must therefore indicate an SWR of $\num{3}$.
