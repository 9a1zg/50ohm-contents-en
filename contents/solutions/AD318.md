First we find the current through the load resistor:

$ I = \frac{U}{R} = \frac{\qty{5}{\volt}}{\qty{10}{\ohm}} = \qty{0.5}{\ampere} $

The voltage difference across the linear regulator is:

$ U_{\mathrm{V}} = \qty{13.8}{\volt} - \qty{5}{\volt} = \qty{8.8}{\volt} $

The power dissipation follows from the voltage drop and the current:

$ P_{\mathrm{V}} = U_{\mathrm{V}} \cdot I = \qty{8.8}{\volt} \cdot \qty{0.5}{\ampere} = \qty{4.4}{\watt} $

The power dissipation in the linear regulator is therefore:

$ P_{\mathrm{V}} = \qty{4.4}{\watt} $
