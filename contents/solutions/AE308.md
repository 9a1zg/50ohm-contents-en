For the bandwidth of the FM signal we use Carson's rule:

$B\approx2 \cdot \left(\Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}} \right)$

The given values are the frequency deviation $\Delta f_{\textrm{T}}=\qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}$ and the maximum modulating frequency $f_{\textrm{mod max}}=\qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz}$.

Substituting gives:

$B\approx2 \cdot \left(\qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}+\qty{2.7}{\kilo\hertz}\right)=2\cdot\qty{5.2}{\kilo\hertz}=\qty{10.4}{\kilo\hertz}$

The bandwidth of the FM signal is therefore approximately $\qty{10.4}{\kilo\hertz}$.
