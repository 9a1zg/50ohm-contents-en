Without the emitter capacitor, the emitter resistor also acts on AC and causes strong negative feedback. The voltage gain can be determined approximately from the ratio of the collector resistor to the emitter resistor:

$v_U \approx \frac{R_C}{R_E} = \frac{\qty{22}{\kilo\ohm}}{\qty{2.2}{\kilo\ohm}} = 10$

The magnitude of the voltage gain therefore falls to approximately $\num{10}$.
