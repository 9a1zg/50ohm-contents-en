The energy stored in a rechargeable battery is calculated as voltage times capacity:

$W = U \cdot Q$

with

$U = \qty{12}{\volt}$

and

$Q = \qty{5}{\ampere\hour}$

This gives:

$W = \qty{12}{\volt} \cdot \qty{5}{\ampere\hour} = \qty{60}{\watt\hour}$

So the battery can store an energy of $\qty{60}{\watt\hour}$.
