Exactly one bit is transmitted per symbol. The data rate is therefore directly equal to the symbol rate:

$R_\mathrm{Daten} = R_\mathrm{Symbol} \cdot \qty{1}{\bit\per{symbol}}$

With a symbol rate of $\qty{45.45}{\baud}$, this gives:

$R_\mathrm{Daten} = \qty{45.45}{\bit\per\second}$

The data rate is therefore $\qty{45.45}{\bit\per\second}$.
