The folded dipole has a feed-point impedance of about $\qtyrange{240}{300}{\ohm}$. The characteristic impedance of the line used is $\qty{75}{\ohm}$.

Since the antenna impedance is greater than the characteristic impedance of the line, the SWR is approximately

$s=\frac{R_\mathrm{a}}{Z}$.

For the two limits of the given range we obtain

$s_\mathrm{min}=\frac{\qty{240}{\ohm}}{\qty{75}{\ohm}}=\num{3.2}$

and

$s_\mathrm{max}=\frac{\qty{300}{\ohm}}{\qty{75}{\ohm}}=\num{4}$.

An SWR of about $\numrange{3.2}{4}$ can therefore be expected on the line.
