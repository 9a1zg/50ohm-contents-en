The SI unit of electric charge is the coulomb ($\unit{\coulomb}$).  
One coulomb is defined as the quantity of charge transported within one second ($\qty{1}{\second}$) when a current of one ampere ($\qty{1}{\ampere}$) flows:  
$\qty{1}{\ampere} \cdot \qty{1}{\second} = \qty{1}{\ampere\second} = \qty{1}{\coulomb}$  
Instead of the coulomb ($\unit{\coulomb}$), $\unit{\ampere\second}$ is often simply used; this also makes checking the units easier.
