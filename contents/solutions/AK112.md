## Formulas needed

Formulas to use from the Federal Network Agency's examination aids for calculating the safety distance in the far field:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot P_{EIRP}}}{E}$ 

and for the relationship between EIRP and ERP:

$P_{EIRP} = P_{ERP} \cdot 10^{\frac{g_d + 2.15 - a}{10}}$

## Data from the question

1. Since it is a parabolic dish: $g_d = \qty{18}{\dBd}$ 
2. The cable loss is: $a = \qty{2}{\dB}$
3. The power is: $P_{ERP} = \qty{40}{\watt}$
4. The limit for the protection of persons is: $E = \qty{61}{\volt\per\meter}$

## Solution steps

1. Calculating $P_{EIRP}$:

$P_{EIRP} = \qty{40}{\watt} \cdot 10^{\frac{18 + 2.15 - 2}{10}} = \qty{2612.52}{\watt}$

2. Calculating the safety distance $d$:

$d = \frac{\sqrt{\qty{30}{\ohm} \cdot \qty{2612.52}{\watt}}}{\qty{61}{\volt\per\meter}} = \frac{\qty{279.96}{\volt}}{\qty{61}{\volt\per\meter}} \approx \qty{4.6}{\meter}$


## Interpretation

The safety distance is $\qty{4.6}{\meter}$.
