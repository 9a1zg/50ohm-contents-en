First, the DC power drawn is calculated from the supply voltage and the current:

$P_\mathrm{DC} = U \cdot I = \qty{12.5}{\volt} \cdot \qty{16}{\ampere} = \qty{200}{\watt}$

The efficiency is the ratio of the RF output power to the DC power drawn:

$\eta = \frac{P_\mathrm{HF}}{P_\mathrm{DC}} = \frac{\qty{90}{\watt}}{\qty{200}{\watt}} = \num{0.45}$

Expressed as a percentage:

$\eta = \num{0.45} \cdot \qty{100}{\percent} = \qty{45}{\percent}$

The efficiency of the amplifier is therefore $\qty{45}{\percent}$.
