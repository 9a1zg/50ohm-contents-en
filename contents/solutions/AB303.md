The aim is to find the size of one segment on the time axis. To do this, look for a signal that goes through one complete period $T$, for example signal $b$. One period consists of eight segments, from which it follows that one segment corresponds to $\frac{T}{8}$, that is $\frac{\qty{360}{\degree}}{8} = \qty{45}{\degree}$ or $\frac{\pi}{4}$. Between the two signals $a$ and $b$ there is a phase shift of exactly one segment, that is $\qty{45}{\degree}$ or $\frac{\pi}{4}$. In other words, signal $b$ leads signal $a$ by $\qty{45}{\degree}$ or $\frac{\pi}{4}$.
