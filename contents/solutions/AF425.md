The operating voltage given is

$ U_\mathrm{CC} = \qty{13.5}{\volt} $

The optimum operating point of the MMIC is at

$ U_D = \qty{4}{\volt} $ and $ I_D = \qty{10}{\milli\ampere} $

The remaining voltage must therefore be dropped across the resistor $R_\text{BIAS}$:

$ U_{R_\text{BIAS}} = U_\mathrm{CC} - U_D $

$ U_{R_\text{BIAS}} = \qty{13.5}{\volt} - \qty{4}{\volt} = \qty{9.5}{\volt} $

Ohm's law gives:

$ R_\text{BIAS} = \frac{U_{R_\text{BIAS}}}{I_D} = \frac{\qty{9.5}{\volt}}{\qty{10}{\milli\ampere}} = \qty{950}{\ohm} $
