The transmit power is increased from $\qty{10}{\watt}$ to $\qty{100}{\watt}$, and so multiplied by ten:

$\frac{\qty{100}{\watt}}{\qty{10}{\watt}} = 10$

Multiplying the power by ten corresponds to an increase in level of $\qty{10}{\decibel}$.

Starting from S8, an increase of $\qty{6}{\decibel}$ first leads to S9. Of the total of $\qty{10}{\decibel}$, $\qty{4}{\decibel}$ then remain.

The S meter reading therefore rises from S8 to *S9 + $\qty{4}{\decibel}$*.
