The common-emitter circuit has a large AC voltage gain, typically in the range $\num{100}\dots\num{300}$. The emitter capacitor bypasses the emitter resistor for AC and so reduces the negative feedback, which results in a high gain.

If the input voltage at the base rises, the collector current increases. As a result, the voltage drop across the collector resistor increases and the output voltage at the collector falls. Input and output signals are therefore in antiphase; the phase shift is $\qty{180}{\degree}$.
