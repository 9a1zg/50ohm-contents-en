What is wanted is a filter that suppresses *harmonics*, that is,
multiples of the transmit frequency. That would be the case with a low-pass
and with a band-pass filter.

The output filters of a transmitter are usually implemented as low-pass
filters, but none of the four possible solutions is a
low-pass filter curve. The four filter curves shown represent
the following filter responses:

* Two different band-stop filters – one with fairly
  steep skirts, one with less steep skirts: the transmitted signal is
  filtered out, and instead frequencies both below and
  above the transmitted signal are passed. Such a filter
  response makes no sense as a transmitter output filter,
  because it would do the opposite of what you
  actually want to achieve.

* A high-pass filter: this filter would pass the wanted signal,
  but the harmonics as well.

* A band-pass filter: it lets the transmitted signal pass and
  suppresses both lower and higher frequencies, and
  thus, of course, the harmonics too.

The correct solution is therefore the band-pass filter curve.
