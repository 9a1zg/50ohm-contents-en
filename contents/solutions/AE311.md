According to Carson's rule, the bandwidth of the FM signal is:

$B\approx2 \cdot \left(\Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}} \right)$

We need the maximum modulating frequency $f_{\textrm{mod max}}$, so we rearrange the formula accordingly:

$\frac{B}{2} = \Delta f_{\textrm{T}} + f_{\textrm{mod max}}$

$f_{\textrm{mod max}} = \frac{B}{2} - \Delta f_{\textrm{T}}$

Substituting the given values gives:

$f_{\textrm{mod max}} = \frac{\qty{10}{\kilo\hertz}}{2} - \qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz} = \qty{5}{\kilo\hertz} - \qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz} = \qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}$

The highest modulating frequency is therefore $\qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}$.
