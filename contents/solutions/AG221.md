The half-power beamwidth, or beamwidth, is measured between the two directions in which the radiated power has fallen to half its maximum value.

The half-power beamwidth is read off at the point $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx \num{0.707}$, that is, point C.  

At this value, the main lobe crosses the diagram at about $\qty{27.5}{\degree}$ and $\qty{-27.5}{\degree}$.

The beamwidth is therefore approximately

$\qty{27.5}{\degree} + \qty{27.5}{\degree} = \qty{55}{\degree}$.

The half-power beamwidth is therefore about $\qty{55}{\degree}$.
