We are given a transformer with a turns ratio of $1:4$ and a resistor $R = \qty{6.4}{\kilo\ohm}$ on the secondary side.

First we determine the impedance ratio. For transformers:

$\left(\frac{N_S}{N_P}\right)^2 = 4^2 = 16$

The impedance on the secondary side is therefore transformed to the primary side by a factor of 16.

What is required is the impedance transformed to the primary side:

$Z_\text{P} = \frac{Z_\text{S}}{16} = \frac{\qty{6.4}{\kilo\ohm}}{16}$

$Z_\text{P} = \qty{0.4}{\kilo\ohm}$
