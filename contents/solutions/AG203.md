The dipole has a total length of about $\qty{40}{\meter}$ and so corresponds approximately to a half-wave dipole for the $\qty{80}{\meter}$ band. Its fundamental frequency is approximately $\qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *a* has $\num{8}$ current antinodes in all. The operating frequency is therefore eight times the fundamental frequency:

$f = 8 \cdot \qty{3.5}{\mega\hertz} = \qty{28}{\mega\hertz}$.

Current curve *a* therefore applies when the dipole is fed at $\qty{28}{\mega\hertz}$.
