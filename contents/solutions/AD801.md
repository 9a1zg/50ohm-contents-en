If you do not immediately recognise the circuit shown as an attenuator
(in this case in the form of a T network), you can also solve the question
by elimination:

* A high-pass or low-pass filter would have to contain a component
  with a frequency-dependent resistance – i.e. a capacitor or an
  inductor. That is not the case here.

* An amplifier would have to contain an active element such as a
  transistor. That is not the case here either.

So, by elimination, only the answer "Attenuator"
is left.
