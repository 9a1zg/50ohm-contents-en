### BFO for SSB

* The beat frequency oscillator (BFO) oscillates exactly on the frequency of the suppressed carrier
* Mixes the carrier back in
* Highest possible frequency stability $\rightarrow$ crystal-controlled oscillator

---
### BFO for CW

* The BFO must be offset from the CW signal
* By the frequency the audible CW tone is to have
* Pleasant tone at $\qtyrange{600}{900}{\hertz}$
* Usually at $\qty{800}{\hertz}$
