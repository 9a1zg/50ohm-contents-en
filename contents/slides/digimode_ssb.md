## Bandwidth of data modes

* Unlike speech, many data modes need less bandwidth
* E.g. BPSK31 with $\qty{31.25}{\hertz}$ or FT8 with $\qty{50}{\hertz}$
* The tones produced are modulated in SSB
* The bandwidth of the transmitted signal stays the same

## Receiving data modes

<left>
* When receiving SSB, several narrowband data modes can be received within the usual bandwidth of $\qty{2.4}{\kilo\hertz}$
* FT8: $\frac{\qty{2400}{\hertz}}{\qty{50}{\hertz}}$ = max. $\num{48}$ signals
* BPSK31: $\frac{\qty{2400}{\hertz}}{\qty{31.25}{\hertz}}$ = max. $\num{76}$ signals
* The wanted data mode signal is then selected on the computer
</left>

## SSTV

<left>
* *Slow-scan television* is the transmission of still pictures by means of data modes
* Pictures transmitted line by line
* Various methods with different resolutions and transmission speeds
* Bandwidth below $\qty{3}{\kilo\hertz}$ and usable on the HF bands
</left>

## ATV
* *Amateur television* is the transmission of moving pictures
* Needs several MHz of bandwidth ($\qty{6}{\mega\hertz}$ and more)
* Therefore only usable from the $\qty{70}{\centi\meter}$ band upwards
