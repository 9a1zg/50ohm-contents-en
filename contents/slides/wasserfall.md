## Reception

* The frequency is set on the radio with a tuning knob or buttons
* Only stations on the frequency set can be heard
* "Tune across the band" slowly to hear other stations

### Waterfall display
 
<left>
[photo:136:n_wasserfall_wasserfall:Display of an ICOM IC-9700. The waterfall is highlighted]

## Difference between oscillogram and amplitude spectrum

* The amplitude spectrum shows amplitudes for different frequencies horizontally
* The oscillogram shows amplitudes at different points in time horizontally
