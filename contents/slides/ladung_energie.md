## Electrical energy

Power times time

$W = P\cdot t$
in joules ($\unit{\joule}$), with time in seconds, or watt-hours ($\unit{\watt\hour}$)

---
[question:AA103]
---
[question:AB502]
---
### Solution
* given: $U = \qty{230}{\volt}$
* given: $I = \qty{0.63}{\ampere}$
* given: $t = \qty{7}{\hour}$
* required: $W$

<fragment>
$$W = P\cdot t = U\cdot I\cdot t = \qty{230}{\volt} \cdot \qty{0.63}{\ampere} \cdot \qty{7}{\hour} = \qty{1.01}{\kilo\watt\hour}$$
</fragment>

[question:AB503]
---
### Solution
* given: $U = \qty{10}{\volt}$
* given: $R = \qty{100}{\ohm}$
* given: $t = \qty{1}{\hour}$
* required: $W$

<fragment>
$\begin{split} W = P\cdot t = \frac{U^2}{R} \cdot t = \frac{(\qty{10}{\volt})^2}{\qty{100}{\ohm}} \cdot \qty{1}{\hour} &= \qty{1}{\watt\hour} \cdot \qty{3600}{\second\per\hour}\\ &= \qty{3600}{\joule}\end{split}$
</fragment>
