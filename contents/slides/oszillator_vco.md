### Feedback
<left>
* Unwanted feedback, e.g. from the RF signal, leads to frequency instability
* This applies to all oscillators
</left>
<right>
<fragment>
Causes:
* Phase shift
* Changes in gain
* Mixing products
* Changes in temperature
</fragment>
</right>
<note>
The causes are not relevant to the examination
</note>
