* *Scatter*: special forms of reflection and scattering of a radio signal
* They allow greater distances to be covered

---
## Rain scatter

<left>
* Scattering by raindrops in all directions (Rayleigh scattering)
* Drop size must suit the wavelength: $\qty{6}{\centi\meter}$ and $\qty{3}{\centi\meter}$ bands
* Antenna is pointed at the rain cloud
* Rough tone in SSB and CW signals (similar to aurora)
</left>

## Backscatter

* Refraction of the sky wave back to the receiver
* Especially during twilight
* Strongly and rapidly fluctuating signal strength (*flutter fading*)

## Aircraft scatter

* Reflection (so not really scatter) of VHF, UHF and SHF from aircraft
* Aircraft must be on the line between transmitter and receiver
* Contact quite short because of the aircraft's fast movement

<note>
Reflection from satellites (e.g. the ISS) is also possible
</note>
