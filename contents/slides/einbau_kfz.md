## Installation
<left>
[photo:64:n_magnetfussantenne:Magnetic-mount antenna on a vehicle roof]
* Ground-plane antenna with the vehicle roof as the counterpoise
</left>
<right>
* As close as possible to the middle of the metal vehicle roof
* Observe the vehicle manufacturer's instructions
* Cables as short as possible and away from other vehicle wiring
</right>
<note>
* Some vehicle manufacturers have documents stating where an antenna should be installed (especially for vehicles used by the police and rescue services)
* A magnetic mount may not hold at high speeds
* The vehicle electronics could suffer interference
</note>

## Caution

<left>
[photo:76:n_Kabelsicherung:Power cable with fuseholder]
</left>
<right>
* A vehicle supply voltage of $\qty{12}{\volt}$ or $\qty{24}{\volt}$ seems harmless
* High currents are possible
* A short circuit can cause an arc, a cable fire or a vehicle fire
* Fit a fuse of the correct rating for the radio
</right>
<note>
* More on this in a later chapter
* Ideally a fuse in both the positive and the negative lead
</note>
