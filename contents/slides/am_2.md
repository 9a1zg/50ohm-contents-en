### Modulation depth

<left>
[picture:24:a_amplitudenmodulation_modulationsgrad1:Amplitude signal at a modulation depth of $\qty{100}{\percent}$]
<fragment>
$m = \frac{\hat{U}_{mod}}{\hat{U}_{T}}$
</fragment>
</left>
<right>
* Ratio between the AF envelope modulated onto the carrier and the RF carrier
* The maximum possible is $m=1$, or $\qty{100}{\percent}$
* Signal swings between the carrier and the maximum possible value
</right>

#### Solution

Read from the graph:
* given: $\hat{U}_{mod} = \qty{1}{\oszidiv} = \qty{3}{\volt}$
* given: ${\hat{U}_{T}} = \qty{2}{\oszidiv} = \qty{6}{\volt}$
* required: $m$

<fragment>
$m = \frac{\hat{U}_{mod}}{\hat{U}_{T}} = \frac{\qty{3}{\volt}}{\qty{6}{\volt}} = 0.5 = \qty{50}{\percent}$
</fragment>

---
### Overmodulation

<left>
[picture:28:a_amplitudenmodulation_modulationsgrad2:Amplitude signal at a modulation depth of more than $\qty{100}{\percent}$]
</left>
<right>
* When $m > 1$
* Causes temporary or complete suppression of the carrier signal
* Leads to distortion and sideband splatter
</right>
