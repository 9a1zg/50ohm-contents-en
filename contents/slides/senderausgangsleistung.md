* Radio amateurs are obliged to keep within the power limits of their radio equipment
* On many amateur bands a *maximum transmitter output power* (*PEP*, peak envelope power) is the limit
* Unwanted emissions also matter

---
### Measuring unwanted emissions

* At the transmitter output
* Including SWR meter, matching unit(s), low-pass filter, etc.
* Measuring unwanted emissions that can reach the antenna

### Measuring the transmitter output power

* Directly at the transmitter output
* Without accessory equipment, filters or cables
* With SSB $\rightarrow$ with modulation
* Single-tone or two-tone drive, but not speech
* Measuring the maximum *envelope power* (PEP)
* Peak power of the transmitter at maximum drive
* Power at the highest peak of an RF oscillation

<note>
* Speech varies
* Relevant to Classes E and A, since their maximum permitted power is given in PEP in Annex 1 to the Amateur Radio Ordinance (Amateurfunkverordnung, AFuV)
</note>
