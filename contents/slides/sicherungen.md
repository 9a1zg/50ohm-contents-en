<left>
* There are what are called miniature fuses in the power supply unit and/or in the lead to the transceiver
* In the event of a fault (short circuit or overload), they interrupt the flow of current
</left>

<right>
[photo:88:n_feinsicherungen:Miniature fuses]
</right>

---

* Once a miniature fuse has blown and the cause has been put right, it has to be replaced.
* Faulty fuses may *only be replaced by fuses of the same type*!
* Both the *current rating* and the *tripping characteristic* have to be taken into account; it states how quickly a fuse blows (fast-acting, medium time-lag, time-lag).

---
