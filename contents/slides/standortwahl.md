* Avoid interaction with other electrical installations and equipment
* Especially in your own home and in the neighbours'
* Interaction can also interfere with your own radio reception
* And, when transmitting, interfere with the working of the equipment
* $\rightarrow$ Mount the antenna outdoors if possible

## Installing an HF antenna

* Best run away from the house at right angles
* The direction of maximum radiation does not point at the building or the neighbours' building
* Less interference from coupling into the wiring in the house

## Installing a directional antenna

* Best as high and as far away as possible
* The field strength in the direction of maximum radiation decreases with distance
