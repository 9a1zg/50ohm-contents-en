## Electromagnetic fields

* Radio waves consist of electric and magnetic fields, which propagate
* Together referred to as *electromagnetic fields*
* Field strengths near antennas may be high!
* Can have a harmful effect on people who are in them

---

* Fields produced at higher transmit powers in particular can harm the human body
* Can also interfere with active implants, e.g. pacemakers or insulin pumps
* Amateur radio installations must not endanger anyone
* *Every radio amateur* must know about the *protection of persons in electromagnetic fields*

## Verification procedure under the BEMFV

* The verification procedure is described in the Ordinance on the "Verification Procedure for Limiting Electromagnetic Fields" (BEMFV)
* *Before putting the station into service, the radio amateur independently ensures and documents that there is no danger to persons*
* Radio amateurs are the only group to enjoy the privilege of carrying out this procedure independently
* All other operators of radio stations must use the BNetzA, for a fee.
