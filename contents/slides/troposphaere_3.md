* In the VHF range, enhanced propagation can occur because of tropospheric inversion layers
* These layers form at the boundaries between warm and cold layers of air
* Radio waves can be reflected by these layers and guided back to the earth over greater distances

---
### Ducting

<left>
[picture:987:a_tropo:Propagation by ducting]
</left>
<right>
* Occurs when radio waves are trapped between two inversion layers
* These inversion layers act like a natural waveguide channel
* Ducting can carry radio waves over distances of more than 1000 km
</right>
