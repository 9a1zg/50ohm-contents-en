* Switching a carrier on and off
* Introduction of a *Morse alphabet* in 1838 by Samuel Morse, optimised by Friedrich Clemens Gerke
* For a long time a Morse test was required for radio amateurs on HF
* Since the mid-1990s, countries have decided whether a *Morse test* is necessary
* The Morse test has been voluntary in Germany only since 2003

<note>
There are countries in which a Morse test is still necessary if you want to use this mode there.
</note>

## Speed
* Morse characters can be sent at different speeds
* Do not overtax the other station with your speed
* Rule of thumb: send no faster than the other station, and no faster than you can receive yourself
