## Loaded potential divider

* The total current rises when the load is increased, i.e. when $R_{\textrm{L}}$ becomes lower
* Assumption: the supply current does not collapse
* When choosing the values, pay attention to the currents through the resistors

---
[question:AD115]
---
#### Solution

In the loaded potential divider, 3 currents flow:
* $I_1$ flows through $R_1$ and causes a power dissipation there of $P_1 = U_1 \cdot I_1 = I_2 \cdot R_1$
* $I_2$ flows through $R_2$ and causes a power dissipation there of $P_2 = U_2 \cdot I_2 = {I_2}^2 \cdot R_2$
* $I_L$ flows through $R_L$ and causes a power dissipation there of $P_L = U_2 \cdot I_L = {I_L}^2 \cdot R_L$
* The current $I_1$ is the sum of $I_2$ and $I_L$ and is therefore the largest current.
