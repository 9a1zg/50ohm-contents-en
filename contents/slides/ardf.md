### Foxhunt (ARDF)

* Another kind of competition is Amateur Radio Direction Finding (ARDF).
* The "foxes" are small hidden transmitters, which the participants have to find with direction-finding receivers and reach on foot.
* The foxes take turns, each transmitting a different one of the following call signs in CW Morse telegraphy: MO, MOE, MOI, MOS, MOH or MO5.
* The winner is whoever is first to find all the foxes and reach the finish
