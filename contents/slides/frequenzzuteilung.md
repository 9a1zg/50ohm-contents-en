* Every frequency use requires a prior *frequency assignment*
* Laid down in the Telecommunications Act (Telekommunikationsgesetz, TKG)
* Individual assignment
  * To a company, e.g. private mobile radio
* General assignment
  * The general public, e.g. amateur radio

---

* Amateur radio may be operated only on the assigned frequencies
* The frequency ranges are agreed internationally
* *But* the national regulations are authoritative

## Frequency ranges for the amateur service

* In Germany, laid down in Annex 1 to the *Amateur Radio Ordinance (Amateurfunkverordnung, AFuV)*
* Transmit only on the frequencies assigned to your certificate class
* Further conditions of use must be complied with
* There are supplementary binding orders and notices
* Published in the Official Gazette and on the website of the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA)
