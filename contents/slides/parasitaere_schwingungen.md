## Parasitic oscillations in RF power amplifiers

* Arise through unwanted feedback
* Causes: capacitive or inductive coupling between components
* No direct relation to the operating frequency of the transmitter
* Show up as power fluctuations when the transmitter is tuned (TX indicator)

### Measures for suppressing parasitic oscillations

<left>
[picture:767:a_parasitaere_schwingungen_parallelwiderstand:Resistor in parallel with the transformer to prevent parasitic oscillations]
</left>
<right>
* In VHF transmitters: ferrite beads on the emitter of the power transistor (close to the transistor)
* In broadband HF output stages: resistor in parallel with the transformer for damping
* Aim: reducing the amplifier's tendency to oscillate
</right>
