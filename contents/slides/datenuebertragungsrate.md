* The *bandwidth* is the frequency range used, in *$\unit{\hertz}$*
* The *data rate* is the amount of data transmitted per unit of time, in *$\unit{\bit\per\second}$*

---

* The data rates achievable in practice differ considerably depending on the transmission method and the propagation conditions.
* Under optimum conditions, Wi-Fi and 5G support data rates up to the range of gigabits per second.
* FT8, on the other hand, can be used even under poor conditions, but transmits only a few bits per second.
