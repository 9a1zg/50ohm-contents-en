* Club station call signs with a 4- to 7-character suffix
* e.g. for historical events, town festivals or sporting events
* The whole amateur radio world can take part in the event
</right>

---

## Rules
* Assigned for a maximum of 1 year by the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA)
* No extension possible
* The suffix can consist of digits and letters
* The last character must always be a letter

---

## International
* There can also be special event stations abroad
* Their call signs may follow different rules
