### 1. Antenna

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Picks up a large number of radio waves
* Passes them on as electrical oscillations
* This type of receiver is called a *tuned radio frequency (TRF) receiver*: the frequency of the signal is not changed on its way to the demodulator
</right>

--- data-transition="none"

### 2. Band-pass filter

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Lets only the wanted frequency range through
* Blocks all other, unwanted frequencies
* The symbol shows two crossed-out waves above and below the wave that is let through
</right>

--- data-transition="none"

### 3. RF amplifier

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Amplifies the signal that has been filtered out
</right>

--- data-transition="none"

### 4. Demodulator

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Recovers the original signal, e.g. a speech signal, from the modulated radio-frequency carrier
* The result is the audio-frequency (AF) signal
</right>

<note>
* The symbol is the circuit symbol of a diode, the most important part of many demodulators
* Covered later in the course
</note>

--- data-transition="none"

### 5. AF amplifier

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Amplifies the demodulated signal
* AF amplifier to amplify the signal for the loudspeaker
</right>

--- data-transition="none"

### 6. Loudspeaker

<left>
[picture:736:aufbau_empfaenger_blockdiagramm:Block diagram of a simple receiver]
</left>
<right>
* Converts the electrical oscillation into a sound wave
* The signal is made audible again
</right>

## Sensitivity

* Receivers have different properties, depending on how they are built
* Important property: *sensitivity*
* Ability to receive weak signals
* The more sensitive the receiver, the weaker the signals it can receive
