<right>
* It is also possible to spread a data stream over several carriers that lie on different but closely spaced frequencies.
* In OFDM, the individual carriers are placed at a spacing at which "crosstalk" is avoided.
</right>
<note>
OFDM: orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing<br/>
Crosstalk: mutual interference between the carriers
</note>

---

* One advantage of this approach is that narrow-band interference affects only one or a few carriers.
* Combined with error correction methods with redundant data transmission, it is thus possible to achieve error-free transmission despite narrow-band interference.

---

---

* A further advantage arises from the lower symbol rate of each individual carrier.
* Because of the lower symbol rate, each symbol lasts longer.
* If there are time shifts due to multipath propagation, the proportion of overlap between the signals is correspondingly smaller.
