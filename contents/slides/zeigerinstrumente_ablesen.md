## Parallax error
<left>
* Avoid parallax error by looking straight at the meter
* Many analogue meters have a mirror behind the needle
* When the needle covers its own mirror image, you are looking straight at it
</left>
<right>
[photo:196:e_parallaxenfehler:Analogue meter with mirror, and parallax error when reading]
</right>
<note>
* Parallax is when you look past an object
