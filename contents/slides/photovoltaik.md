#### Solution
* given: $U_0 = \qty{0.6}{\volt}$
* given: $I_k = \qty{1}{\ampere}$
* given: $N_R = 30, N_P = 4$
* required: $U_{0,\mathrm{ges}}, I_{k,\mathrm{ges}}$

<fragment>
$U_{0,\mathrm{ges}} = N_R \cdot U_0 = 30 \cdot \qty{0.6}{\volt} = \qty{18}{\volt}$
</fragment>
<fragment>
$I_{0,\mathrm{ges}} = N_P \cdot I_k = 4 \cdot \qty{1}{\ampere} = \qty{4}{\ampere}$
</fragment>
