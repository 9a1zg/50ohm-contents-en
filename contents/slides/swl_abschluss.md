# You are ready to listen

You now know about:

- receivers, antennas and propagation
- electrical and mechanical safety
- call signs, bands and modes
- operating practice, the log and QSL cards
- the legal limits of reception
- the way to your own DE identifier

--- style="font-size: smaller;"


# Your next steps

1. Get in touch with your local club.
2. Set up your receiver and antenna safely.
3. Listen on different amateur bands.
4. Keep a careful log.
5. Practise with the question catalogue and the practice examinations.
6. Take the DE examination and apply for your identifier.

---

# And then?

With QSL cards, the DLD-SWL and perhaps later the *Deutscher Empfangsmeister* (German Reception Master), many goals remain open.

And if you want to transmit yourself, the Class N amateur radio examination is the next possible step.
