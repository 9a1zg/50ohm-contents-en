## Tolerance

* Deviation from the actual value
* Example: silver means $\qty{\pm 10}{\percent}$
* $\qty{10}{\percent} \cdot \qty{47}{\kilo\ohm} = \qty{4.7}{\kilo\ohm}$
* Resistance value between $\qty{42.3}{\kilo\ohm}$ and $\qty{51.7}{\kilo\ohm}$
