## Amateur bands

* Bands are usually named after their approximate wavelength
* The higher the frequency, the shorter the wavelength
* Example: the range around $\qty{3.6}{\mega\hertz}$ is called the $\qty{80}{\meter}$ band

<fragment>
$\lambda[\unit{\meter}] = \dfrac{300}{f[\unit{\mega\hertz}]} \qquad f[\unit{\mega\hertz}] = \dfrac{300}{\lambda[\unit{\meter}]}$
</fragment>

<note>
The formulas are a memory aid. For reception, what matters most is matching the frequency range and the band name reliably.
</note>
