### Address and changes

* Changes of name or address must be notified to the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA) without delay *after* the change
* That way the BNetzA can reach you by post at any time

* If you are unsure of the name of a German station you are in contact with, you can search for the call sign on the BNetzA website
* It displays the call sign, the name and, unless the holder has objected, the address
* The same information can also be found in the call sign list, which can be downloaded as a PDF document
