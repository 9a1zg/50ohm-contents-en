## Horn antennas and waveguides
<left>
Picture of a horn antenna to follow
</left>
<right>
* Horn antennas can be fed by a waveguide
* In waveguides, i.e. metal tubes, the microwave energy is guided by reflection from the walls
</right>

---
## Waveguides in detail

* Waveguides guide microwaves by reflecting them from their metal walls
* In this way the wave reaches a horn, from which it is radiated, or is fed into the waveguide

## Gain of a parabolic dish

$g_i = 10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\left(\frac{\pi \cdot d}{\lambda}\right)^2 \cdot \eta\right)}\unit{\dBi}$

* Calculated with the formula in the formula sheet
* Depends on the diameter
* Usually very high gain

---
[question:AG226]
---
#### Solution
<left>
* given: $d = \qty{30}{\centi\meter}$
* given: $\eta_{eff} = 1$
</left>
<right>
* given: $f = \qty{5.7}{\giga\hertz}$
* required: $g_i$
</right>

<fragment>
$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{\qty{3\cdot 10^8}{\meter\per\second}}{\qty{5.7}{\giga\hertz}} = \qty{0.053}{\meter}$
</fragment>
<fragment>
$\begin{split}g_i &= 10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\left(\frac{\pi \cdot d}{\lambda}\right)^2 \cdot \eta\right)}\unit{\dBi}\\ &= 10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\left(\frac{\pi \cdot \qty{0.3}{\meter}}{\qty{0.053}{\meter}}\right)^2 \cdot 1\right)} \unit{\dBi}\\ &= \qty{25.1}{\dBi}\end{split}$
</fragment>
