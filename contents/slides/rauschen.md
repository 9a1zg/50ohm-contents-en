* The audible noise depends on the bandwidth of the receiver
* Noise power can be calculated from the bandwidths
* E.g. when using different filters

<fragment>
$\Delta P_R = \qty{10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\frac{B_1}{B_2}\right)}}{\dB}$
</fragment>

#### Solution
* given: $B_1 = \qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}$
* given: $B_2 = \qty{0.5}{\kilo\hertz}$
* required: $\Delta P_R$

<fragment>
$\begin{split}\Delta P_R &= \qty{10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\frac{B_1}{B_2}\right)}}{\dB}\\ &= \qty{10 \cdot \log_{10}{\left(\frac{\qty{2.5}{\kilo\hertz}}{\qty{0.5}{\kilo\hertz}}\right)}}{\dB}\\ &\approx \qty{7}{\dB}\end{split}$
</fragment>
