* To bypass filters, some FM radios have a separate port for data modes
* This is often labelled *DATA* or *9600*
* 9600 corresponds to the data rate in baud ($\unit{\baud}$) that can be transmitted with it
* The computer's TNC (Terminal Node Controller) is connected directly to it
* Today often implemented directly as a USB port
