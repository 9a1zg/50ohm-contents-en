## Circuit

* When an electrical load is connected to the terminals of a voltage source, the charges start to move
* This is a *closed circuit*
* Depending on the voltage and the load, more or less current flows
* The *electric current* is measured in amperes ($\unit{\ampere}$)

<note>
Named after the French physicist and mathematician André-Marie Ampère, who gave a scientific explanation of voltage and current in the 1820s
</note>
