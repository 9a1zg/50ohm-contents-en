#### Example

A radio with $\qty{5}{\watt}$ transmit power may be operated with a gain factor of $\num{1.8}$ relative to the isotropic radiator:<br/>$\qty{5}{\watt} \cdot 1.8 = \qty{9}{\watt}$ EIRP

<note>
That ERP is used for one and EIRP for the other is (probably) connected with the antennas mainly used on the bands available to Class N. The BNetzA has not given a reason.
</note>
