* *Automatic level control (ALC)* controls the drive to the transmitter power amplifier
* Reduces the amplitude in the transmit path when it is overdriven
* Not to be confused with the AGC (automatic gain control) in the receive path

---
### How it works
* Senses the output power and compares it with a preset maximum value
* When this is exceeded, a control voltage is applied to the preceding RF amplifier stage
* The amplitude of the transmitted signal is reduced

---
* If the ALC indicator responds, the transmitter is being overdriven by too strong an AF signal
* With SSB, slight overdriving is desirable
* Evens out variations in the loudness of the voice
* The ALC indicator is therefore often a bar graph

---
* With digital modes the ALC should not respond
* Distortion of the transmitted signal
* Set the AF drive so that the ALC just does not respond
