## Directional properties

* *Isotropic radiator*: a hypothetical antenna that radiates equally strongly in all directions
* A real antenna has directional properties
* Stronger than the isotropic radiator in certain directions
* Weaker than the isotropic radiator in certain directions
* The *direction of maximum radiation* is the direction with the greatest antenna gain

---
## Gain in dBi

* Gain in one direction relative to the isotropic radiator
* Can be given in $\unit{\dB}$
* With the isotropic radiator as the reference, *$\unit{\dBi}$* is used

## Gain of a half-wave dipole

* At right angles to the conductor, a half-wave dipole radiates $\qty{2.15}{\dB}$ more strongly than an isotropic radiator
* The gain is $\qty{2.15}{\dBi}$

---
## Gain in dBd

* For other antennas, the gain relative to a half-wave dipole is of interest
* With the half-wave dipole as the reference, *$\unit{\dBd}$* is used
* A half-wave dipole has a gain of $\qty{0}{\dBd}$ and $\qty{2.15}{\dBi}$ in the direction of maximum radiation
