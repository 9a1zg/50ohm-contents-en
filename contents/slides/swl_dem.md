# Deutscher Empfangsmeister

<right>
The **Deutscher Empfangsmeister (DEM)**, the German Reception Master, is a DARC award for many years of varied activity as a listening amateur.

# DEM in Bronze

The requirements include, among other things:

- membership of the DARC or the VFDB
- at least one year of SWL activity
- a DE identifier
- reception capability on five amateur bands
- the DLD-SWL 100
- the required confirmed reception reports

---

# Further levels

For DEM in Silver and Gold, the requirements for duration, awards and confirmed reception reports increase.

Holders of the title receive an identifier from a **DE0 range**.

You can find the current conditions at:

[50ohm.de/swl](https://50ohm.de/swl/)
