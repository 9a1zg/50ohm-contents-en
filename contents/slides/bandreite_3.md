* Bandwidth according to the Amateur Radio Ordinance (Amateurfunkverordnung, AFuV): mean transmit power in the part of the band transmitted
* $\qty{99}{\percent}$ of the transmit power must lie within the required bandwidth
* At most $\qty{0.5}{\percent}$ may fall in the adjacent ranges below and above
</right>
<note>
Particularly important for FM emissions (deviation!).<br/>
Measure with a spectrum analyser
