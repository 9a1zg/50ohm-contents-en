<left>
Wave propagation in air and in a vacuum:
$\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f}$
</left>
<right>
* Lines and antenna wires need a correction factor
* The *velocity factor* $k_\mathrm{v}$ (for antennas: *shortening factor*)
* About $\qty{95}{\percent}$ of propagation in a vacuum
* $\lambda_\mathrm{Leitung} = k_\mathrm{v} \cdot \dfrac{c}{f}$
</right>
<note>
* The wave travels more slowly in the cable
* Coaxial cables are covered later
</note>

* For resonance, wire antennas must be shortened by approx. $\qty{5}{\percent}$
