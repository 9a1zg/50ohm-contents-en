The front part of a call sign contains a *country prefix*. It helps the SWL to place the station heard geographically. The assignment is regulated internationally by the ITU.

For the DE examination, these European country prefixes are particularly important:

You do not have to learn a complete list of country prefixes by heart. When listening in practice, it is worth looking up new prefixes and noting them in the log together with the country. That way your knowledge grows with every station you hear.

<attention>
If a station gives a call sign with a foreign country prefix and a stroke in front of it, it may be temporarily in another country. What counts is always the complete call sign as heard.
</attention>
