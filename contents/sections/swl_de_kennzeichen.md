A *DE identifier* [index:DE identifier] identifies a qualified German receiving station within the DARC and the VFDB. It consists of the prefix *DE*, a digit and three letters, for example *DE1SWL*.

<margin>
| l: Range | X: Use |
| DE0AAA to DE0PZZ | *Deutscher Empfangsmeister* (German Reception Master) |
| DE0QAA to DE0QZZ | Training receiving stations in local clubs and schools |
| DE0RAA to DE0ZZZ | *Deutscher Empfangsmeister* |
| DE1AAA to DE9ZZZ | Short-wave listeners |
[table:swl_de_kennzeichen_bereiche:Ranges of the DE identifier system]
</margin>

The identifier is issued by the DARC head office on application, documented in a certificate and is not transferable. It may be used only once the certificate has been received.

Three letters can be proposed for a personal preferred identifier. There is no entitlement to it. Allocation depends on availability and on the allocation scheme.

<attention>
A DE identifier is unique worldwide, but it is not an amateur call sign and not a permission to transmit.
</attention>

The basic requirement for the assignment is membership of the DARC or the VFDB and one of the qualifications provided for: a passed DE examination, the title *Deutscher Empfangsmeister* or an existing amateur radio licence. If the holder leaves the association, the assignment lapses.
