In this chapter we have already met most of the basic components in a circuit diagram. Three important components are still missing, however, whose circuit symbols you are expected to recognise in the examination. How exactly these components work is only covered in Classes E and A.

Figure [ref:n_bauelemente_kondensator] shows various capacitors. A capacitor stores a small amount of energy. It consists, for example, of two parallel plates. This can also be seen clearly in the circuit symbol.

Figure [ref:n_bauelemente_spule] shows various inductors. Like the capacitor, an inductor stores a small amount of energy, but technically it works in a completely different way from the capacitor. In the simplest case, an inductor consists of a wound coil of wire. The circuit symbol is modelled on it.
