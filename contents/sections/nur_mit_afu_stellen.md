The amateur service is restricted to radio amateurs. An amateur station may therefore only contact other amateur stations. It is not permitted to communicate with stations of other radiocommunication services. So from an amateur station you may not, for example, communicate with public authorities, with aeronautical or maritime radio stations, or with a CB station. 

<indepth>
Radio amateurs may of course also take part in *CB radio* [index:CB radio:Taking part in]. To do so, however, they must – like everyone else – use an approved CB radio.
</indepth>

Furthermore, it is in principle not permitted to pass on messages from or to people who are not radio amateurs. For example, greetings may not be sent to a radio amateur's relatives if they are not radio amateurs themselves. The only exception is emergencies and disasters [index:Emergency communications]. In those cases, messages may be sent from and to people who are not radio amateurs.
