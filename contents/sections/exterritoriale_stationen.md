If a German amateur station is operated outside the sovereign territory of the Federal Republic of Germany, and no other country has sovereign territory at that location, it is called an "extraterritorial station". Such stations are assigned a call sign from the block DP0AA to DP2ZZZ.

Examples of extraterritorial stations are the International Space Station (ISS), the Neumayer III research station in the Antarctic and the research vessel Polarstern, with the call signs DP0ISS, DP0GVN and DP0POL.
