In Class E we have already met aurora: when charged particles from the solar wind couple into the upper atmosphere, they produce aurorae on the one hand, and on the other they refract radio waves, which is used for DX contacts above all in the VHF range ($\qty{6}{\meter}$ and $\qty{2}{\meter}$). In Class A there are further, more detailed questions on this.

You should remember the following points: 

* Aurora occurs in the E region ($\num{90}$ to $\qty{130}{\kilo\meter}$) near the poles.
* Charged particles from the sun enter the atmosphere.
* For us in Europe, the polar region is to the north.
* CW is best suited to aurora.
* The signal is "fluttering" and "buzzing".
