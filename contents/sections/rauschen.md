The noise audible in a receiver fundamentally depends on the receiver's bandwidth and is proportional to it. So if you double the bandwidth, the noise power doubles too.

[question:AB408]

To calculate the change in level of the noise power ($p_\text{r}$) for two given bandwidths, we use the following formula from the formula sheet:

$\Delta P_\text{R} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{B_1}{B_2}\right)\unit{\dB}$

Here $B_1$ and $B_2$ stand for the two receiver bandwidths to be compared, which are expressed as a ratio of one to the other.
