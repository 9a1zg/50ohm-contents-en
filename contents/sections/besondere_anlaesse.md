For special occasions of general significance, such as historical events, town festivals and sporting events, radio amateurs in Germany can apply for club station call signs with a suffix of four to seven characters. In this way, the whole amateur radio world can take part in an event.

These special club station call signs are assigned by the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA) for a maximum of one year. An extension is not possible. Unlike the call signs mentioned so far, the suffix can consist of digits and letters. The last character must always be a letter. Combinations such as DP44N44T are therefore also possible.

Radio amateurs can operate special event stations both nationally and internationally. Abroad, however, the rules may differ; in other countries, for example, even longer call signs are possible.
