The antenna performs the real "magic" of radio: when transmitting, it gives off electrical oscillations as radio waves, which spread out into the distance. Conversely, when receiving, radio waves from far away reach the antenna. The antenna picks up the radio waves and passes them on as electrical oscillations via the antenna cable to the radio.
