Unfortunately, amateur radio does not come without financial contributions. Under the Telecommunications Act (Telekommunikationsgesetz, TKG) and the Electromagnetic Compatibility Act (EMVG), radio amateurs have to pay annual contributions, which are laid down in detail in the Frequency Protection Contribution Ordinance (FSBeitrV). The FSBeitrV restricts payment to radio amateurs who hold a licence. The demand for payment comes as a fee notice from the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA).

Besides the regular fees, further fees are charged, which are governed by the "Special Fees Ordinance of the BNetzA (BNetzABGebV)". They include, for example, fees for the amateur radio examinations and, of course, the fee for the issue of the licence.

If you do not pay a fee, the state collects it under the Administrative Enforcement Act (Verwaltungs-Vollstreckungsgesetz, VwVG). Under this Act, the BNetzA has various means of enforcement.
