Antennas on the roof or outside a building are at risk from direct lightning strikes. When erecting antennas, protective measures must therefore be taken that comply with the *recognised codes of practice*. The Association for Electrical, Electronic and Information Technologies (Verband der Elektrotechnik Elektronik und Informationstechnik e. V.), VDE for short, publishes standards that document the current state of the art. If you follow the *VDE standards*, it is easy to show that you have complied with the recognised codes of practice.
