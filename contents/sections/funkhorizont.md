Radio waves in the VHF and UHF ranges behave in a similar way to light. Light reaches at most as far as the geographical (visible) horizon. Radio waves manage approx. $\qty{15}{\percent}$ more range, because they follow the curvature of the earth a little (Figure [ref:n_funkhorizont]). We then speak of the *radio horizon* [index:Radio horizon].

For a reliable contact in the VHF and UHF ranges, and especially on even higher frequencies, a line of sight [index:Line of sight] is usually needed. Tall buildings or mountains can impair the contact. The higher the antenna, the greater the range. Distant stations are easier to reach from a high mountain than from a valley or a town centre.
