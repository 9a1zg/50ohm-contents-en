As we learnt at the beginning of the course, every radio amateur licensed in Germany in Class E or A is automatically also an instructor. By using the call sign suffix "/T" or "/Trainee", any personal call sign becomes a training call sign. The same applies to club station call signs. With it, the trainee may operate under supervision and within the instructor's scope of privileges.

Training operation is intended for people who do not hold a corresponding amateur radio certificate. The training call sign may not be used by the instructor for their own transmissions. Training operation serves as practical preparation for taking the examination of competence to obtain an amateur radio certificate.

[question:VD301]

The instructor must always stay in the immediate vicinity of the trainee and guide them in operating the radio and in operating procedure. Only by staying in the immediate vicinity can the instructor step in and regulate the training operation. In an extreme case, this can also mean switching off the transmitter. Supervising a trainee over a greater distance, or by radio from another location, is not permitted, because direct guidance and supervision are then not ensured.

If the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA) requests it, the instructor must provide the authority with information on the "type and scope" of the training operation.
