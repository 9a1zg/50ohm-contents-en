The bandwidth of a signal or an emission is measured in hertz ($\unit{\hertz}$). The bandwidth of a signal is found by measuring the difference between the highest and the lowest transmit frequency of the emission.

<indepth>
So the bandwidth indicates how much spectrum the signal takes up. We usually measure the bandwidth of a signal between the frequency points at which the spectral power has fallen to $\frac{1}{2}$ (corresponding to $\qty{-3}{\dB}$) of the peak value.
