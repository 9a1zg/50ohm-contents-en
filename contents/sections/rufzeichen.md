Radio stations use call signs [index:Call sign] to identify themselves. Call signs consist of a sequence of letters and digits. For example, every aircraft or ship has its own call sign if it is equipped with radio. On aircraft, the call sign can even be seen from outside. The aircraft in Figure [ref:rufzeichen_flugzeug], for example, has the call sign DEBPF. The purpose of call signs is to make other radio stations easy to recognise.

Every radio amateur, too, is assigned a personal call sign [index:Call sign:Personal] that is unique worldwide. Radio amateurs must give their call sign at the beginning and end of every contact, and repeat it every 10 minutes during longer contacts.
