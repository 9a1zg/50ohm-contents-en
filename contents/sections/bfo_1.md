A *beat frequency oscillator*, *BFO* for short, generates an auxiliary carrier so that the IF signal of a receiver can be demodulated with it by means of a mixer. That is why it is also called a carrier insertion oscillator. This technique is used above all for receiving signals without an auxiliary carrier (SSB and CW). The BFO must be set for the best possible intelligibility of the received signal.
