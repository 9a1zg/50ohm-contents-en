If you operate from a location other than the home location given in the licence document, you may use a call sign suffix voluntarily. In training operation, the corresponding suffix is mandatory when the trainee uses the instructor's personal call sign. Table [ref:n_rufzeichenzusaetze] lists the official suffixes that can be appended to the call sign for this. Other suffixes may not be used. 

| l: Suffix | l: Spoken | X: Meaning |
| am | aeronautical mobile | On board an aircraft in flight |
| mm | maritime mobile | On board a ship at sea |
| m | mobile | From a land vehicle or from a ship on inland waters |
| p | portable | On foot or at a temporary fixed location |
| R | Remote | Remote operation |
| T | Trainee | Training operation |
[table:n_rufzeichenzusaetze:Possible call sign suffixes]


The suffix is appended to a call sign with a "/". In voice operation, the suffix can be spoken straight after the call sign, or appended to it with the word "Stroke". Table [ref:n_rufzeichenzusaetze_sprechweise] gives examples of how they are written and spoken.

| l: Written | X: Pronunciation |
| DL1FLO/m | Delta Lima One Foxtrot Lima Oscar (Stroke) Mobile |
| DM4EAX/p | Delta Mike Four Echo Alpha X-Ray (Stroke) Portable |
| DL1ASN/mm | Delta Lima One Alpha Sierra November (Stroke) Maritime Mobile |
| DG2RON/am | Delta Golf Two Romeo Oscar November (Stroke) Aeronautical Mobile |
[table:n_rufzeichenzusaetze_sprechweise:How call sign suffixes are spoken; "Stroke" is optional and can be left out]

If you are on board an aircraft, i.e. an aeroplane, hot-air balloon, zeppelin or similar, and want to operate, you can append the suffix /am (aeronautical mobile) to your own call sign. The aircraft must be completely in the air and may have no connection to the ground, e.g. through tethers. Operation must be permitted by the pilot in command before it begins, but need not be authorised by the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA).

Something similar to aircraft applies to vessels. If you are on the water in a motor boat, a sailing yacht or another vessel outside the 12-mile zone, in international waters, the suffix "/mm" (maritime mobile) may be appended. If you are within the 12-mile zone or on rivers, lakes or similar inland waters, "/m" (mobile) may be used. The master must give consent before you transmit.

If you set up your station temporarily at a location that is not entered in the assignment document, for example for amateur radio activity out in the countryside, "/p" (Stroke Portable) can be added to the call sign. The same applies when you move about with a portable radio.
