As we learnt in the introduction, only radio amateurs may transmit on the amateur frequencies. There is one exception to this: training operation [index:Training operation]. It allows people who are not radio amateurs to transmit for training purposes under the direct guidance and supervision of a licensed Class E or Class A radio amateur.

[question:VD303]

The trainee uses the instructor's call sign and adds the suffix "/T". This suffix is spoken as "Stroke Trainee" or simply "Trainee". A trainee of DG2RON, for example, gives the call sign as "DG2RON Trainee". This lets others see that a trainee is transmitting.
