A *hop* is the path of the sky wave of a radio signal from the transmitting antenna until it returns to the earth's surface, with refraction at the ionosphere having taken place in between. The distance covered can be determined purely geometrically and depends on the height of the refracting region and on the angle (to the earth's surface) at which the signal is radiated from the transmitting antenna. The lower this angle and the higher the refracting region, the greater the skip distance.
