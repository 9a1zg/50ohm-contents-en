For the following question it helps to know that the cable loss increases with rising frequency. The case with the highest attenuation is therefore the application with the highest frequency (corresponding to the shortest wavelength).

The dielectrics and plastic sheaths in particular contribute to the attenuation of RF signals. Twin feeders with as little dielectric as possible between the conductors therefore have particularly low loss.
