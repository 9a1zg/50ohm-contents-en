Radiocommunication services are defined internationally in the Radio Regulations (RR). They include, for example, the maritime mobile service, the broadcasting service and also the amateur service. Radio applications under a general assignment (CB radio, Wi-Fi, etc.) may also be regulated internationally, but they are not a radiocommunication service under the ITU Radio Regulations (RR).

Since amateur radio, too, is an official radiocommunication service, it involves meaningful and responsible tasks. They range from training, through technical investigations, to intercommunication between radio amateurs. That last point should not be underestimated. Our contacts promote international understanding. Amateur radio via satellites, too, does not differ in its purpose from the rest of amateur radio.
