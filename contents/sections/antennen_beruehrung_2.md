When transmitting, high voltages arise on every antenna. These voltages are not evenly distributed. On the classic dipole, voltage antinodes form at both ends, and they present a considerable risk of injury even at low transmit powers of a few watts.

Where the voltage antinodes form depends on the type of antenna (monoband antenna, multiband antenna, etc.).

You do not touch a transmitting antenna in operation!
