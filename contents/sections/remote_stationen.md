Under Section 2 no. 6a of the Amateur Radio Ordinance (Amateurfunkverordnung, AFuV), the remote station is defined as follows: "*Remote operation: the unattended, remotely controlled operation of a fixed amateur station under the continuous, indirect and complete control of a radio amateur authorised to participate in the amateur service, including the setting of the frequencies used for transmitting and receiving and the determination of the time and duration of the amateur radio transmissions.*"

The use of remote stations allows Class A radio amateurs to operate a station at another location, for example when no station of their own can be set up at home. All operation – switching the transmitter on and off, setting the frequency, transferring the transmit and receive signals – is carried out remotely, e.g. via the internet. Sharing a remote station is possible under certain circumstances, which we will come to.

Remote operation must be notified to the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA) by the operator of the station, with an operating notification and the operator's contact details. During operation, the operator must be reachable by the BNetzA under the contact details given. 

[question:VD602]
[question:VD608]

Continuous, indirect and complete control of the station must be ensured throughout the entire operating time. Indirect means here that control may be exercised by means of aids or helpers, and that you do not have to be on site in person.

In practice, you must make sure that the remote station can be put into a safe state in the event of faults, such as loss of connection, for example by switching off the mains supply.

To use another radio amateur's remote station, you need the permission of its operator. The operator must ensure that only radio amateurs authorised by the operator can use the remote station.

Class A club stations may also be operated as remote stations. Access to the club station in remote operation must, however, be limited to the members of the group of radio amateurs that operates the club station.

Training operation is also possible at a remote station. The same rules apply as for training operation in general. The trainee must be guided and supervised directly, that is, in person. "/T" or "/Trainee" must be appended to the personal call sign or to the club station call sign.
