A linear amplifier maps the input signal exactly (linearly) onto an output signal, but enlarged by the corresponding gain factor (increase in amplitude). The waveforms are preserved between the input and the output of the amplifier. There is no distortion due to non-linearities.
A linear amplifier is needed for all modes that carry modulation information in their amplitude or phase (e.g. SSB, AM, QAM, QPSK, PSK etc.).
