Even a receiver needs electrical power. Power supply units, multi-socket extension leads and supply leads must be undamaged and suitable for the intended current. Repairs to equipment that carries mains voltage should be left to qualified people.

---

*Miniature fuses* protect a piece of equipment or a circuit in the event of a short circuit or overload. When a fuse is replaced, the current rating and tripping characteristic must match those specified for the replacement. A fuse with a higher rating can allow leads and components to overheat.

[question:NK305]

<attention>
Never bridge a fuse. Before changing one, switch the equipment off and disconnect it completely from the power supply. If the new fuse blows again, the cause must be found.
</attention>

Rechargeable batteries may only be charged with a suitable charger. Damaged or swollen batteries should no longer be used. Cables should be laid so that nobody trips over them and no connector is put under mechanical strain.

An outdoor antenna should not be used during a thunderstorm. Antenna feeders should only be disconnected if this can be done safely and in good time before the thunderstorm.
