Welcome to the fascinating world of amateur radio! The amateur service not only offers the opportunity to extend your own knowledge through experiments and continuous further education, but also makes it possible to contribute to global understanding. In addition, the amateur service can support relief operations in emergencies and disasters. It is important to stress that the amateur service stands on an equal footing with other radio services such as mobile telephony, broadcasting and aeronautical radio. As a participant in the amateur service, you have the right to develop and operate your own transmitting and receiving equipment. Amateur radio opens up the possibility of experimenting with a wide variety of transmission methods – from classic short wave through satellite communication to the Internet of Things, including new methods using artificial intelligence.

Participation in the amateur service requires an amateur radio certificate, which is obtained by passing an examination at the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur, BNetzA). In Germany, amateur radio is divided into three classes: Class N for newcomers, the extended Class E, and Class A with the highest power on all allocated frequencies.

This platform serves as preparation for the examinations for the various amateur radio classes. It should be borne in mind that candidates come with different prior knowledge. For some it is advisable to take all the examinations for the various classes one after the other, while others prefer the direct route to Class A. Some find enough support in self-study with a book or an online course, while others prefer a face-to-face course or a personal contact person. This platform is designed to cater for all these different learning styles.
