1) Short description: Diagram of the electromagnetic spectrum with the amateur radio bands between "300 MHz" (bottom) and "3 THz" (top); with three vertical scales: a scale for the names of the ranges "UHF", "SHF", "EHF" and "THF"; a scale for the frequency bands giving the upper and lower frequency in MHz or GHz; a scale for the wavelength in cm or mm from "70 cm" (bottom) to "1.2 mm" (top).

2) Detailed description: The diagram is arranged vertically and consists of three separate scales. The left-hand scale contains – seen from the bottom – the names of the ranges "UHF" (300 MHz–3 GHz), "SHF" (3 GHz–30 GHz), "EHF" (30 GHz–300 GHz) and "THF" (300 GHz–3 THz). In the middle is a scale with the amateur radio frequency bands: the upper frequencies are on the left, the lower frequencies on the right. The upper frequencies are shifted slightly upwards relative to the lower frequencies, depending on the width of the band.
