Brief summary: Block diagram of a radio installation with antenna, RF amplifier, RX and TX mixers, driver, PA, a TRX for 28–30 MHz and a 116 MHz feed from a generator "38.666 MHz" via a block "1/3".

Detailed description: On the left is an antenna symbol, connected to a line and a small changeover switch labelled "RX" and "TX". From the switch a line with an arrow leads at the top to the right through a block "RF" ("HF") (amplifier symbol) to a block "RX mixer" ("RX‑Mischer") (square with circle/cross). To the right of it is a further small changeover switch labelled "RX" and "TX", which leads to a rectangular block "TRX"; beside it is "28–30 MHz". At the top right is text: "RX:" and below it "144 MHz – 116 MHz = 28 MHz" and "146 MHz – 116 MHz = 30 MHz". At the bottom the transmit branch runs from the right-hand "RX/TX" switch to the left into a block "TX mixer" ("TX‑Mischer") (square with circle/cross), on into "Driver" ("Treiber") (amplifier symbol) and into "PA", then back to the left-hand switch and the antenna. In the middle at the bottom is a block labelled "G" with a sine line; below it is "38.666 MHz". From there an arrow points to the right to a block marked "1" above "3". From this block a vertical line labelled "116 MHz" leads with arrows up to the "RX mixer" and down to the "TX mixer". All connections are drawn as lines with arrowheads that indicate the direction of the signal.
