1) Short description: Schematic representation of a vertical antenna ("mech. 5/8 λ") with an inserted inductor and four horizontal radials ("λ/4").

2) Detailed description: The figure shows the schematic representation of a vertical radiator with an inductor inserted at its lower end. The radiator is connected to the inner conductor of a coaxial cable leading downwards. From the outer conductor of the coaxial cable four horizontal conductors (radials) run outwards at intervals of 90°. Parallel to the vertical radiator there is an arrow pointing up and down, labelled "mech. 5/8 λ". Parallel to one of the radials an arrow pointing inwards and outwards, labelled "λ/4", can be seen.
