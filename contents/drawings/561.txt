1) Short description: Circuit symbol with a circle, one connection on the left and two connections on the right.

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line in its lower part. This connection has an arrow pointing towards the line. From the vertical line two parallel horizontal lines go to the right inside the circle. The upper one ends at a short vertical connection upwards, the lower one at a short vertical connection downwards.
