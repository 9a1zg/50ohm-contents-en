1) Short description: Semicircular, multicoloured graphic of the layers of the ionosphere above the earth; on the left the marking "Height [km]" with the marks "up to 10", "50-90", "90-130" and "130-400"; on the right the marking of the layers "Troposphere", "D region", "E region" and "F_1 + F_2 regions"; vertical double-headed arrow "Ionosphere" from the lower boundary of the D region to the upper boundary of the F_1 + F_2 regions; within the E region an elliptical figure labelled "Sporadic-E".

2) Detailed description: The semicircular, multicoloured graphic shows the concentrically arranged, arc-shaped layers of the ionosphere above the earth, which is drawn in blue at the bottom. At the left-hand end of the layers there is the marking "Height [km]" with the marks "up to 10", "50-90", "90-130" and "130-400". At the right-hand end are the names of the layers "Troposphere", "D region", "E region" and "F_1 + F_2 regions". A vertical double-headed arrow labelled "Ionosphere" reaches from the lower boundary of the D region to the upper boundary of the F_1 + F_2 regions. Within the E region there is an elliptical figure labelled "Sporadic-E".
