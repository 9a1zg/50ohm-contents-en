Short description: Circuit diagram with a bipolar transistor, several resistors and capacitors, supply terminals "+" and "−", labels "U_E", "I_B", "I_C", "U_CE", "U_A" and two small time diagrams for "U_E(t)" and "I_B(t)".

Detailed description: Two horizontal conductors run at the top and at the bottom; on the right they are labelled "+" (top) and "−" (bottom). At top left a capacitor is drawn, its upper end on the upper conductor. In the middle on the left a coupling capacitor leads from a left-hand open terminal (small open circle) to a node. Four elements meet at this node: the base of the bipolar transistor in the middle (base on the left, collector at the top, emitter at the bottom), a resistor upwards to the "+" conductor, a resistor downwards to the "−" conductor, and the left-hand coupling capacitor. At the base input an arrow labelled "I_B" points to the base. The transistor's collector is taken upwards via a vertical resistor to the "+" conductor. From the collector a further coupling capacitor goes to the right to a right-hand open terminal (small open circle). Next to the collector a downward-pointing arrow with "I_C" is drawn. The emitter leads downwards to an earth symbol, which is connected to the lower "−" conductor. To the left of the left-hand coupling capacitor is a downward-pointing arrow with "U_E". To the right of the transistor is a downward-pointing arrow with "U_CE". At the far right, next to the right-hand coupling capacitor, is a downward-pointing arrow with "U_A". At the bottom two small diagrams are shown: on the left a sketch of axes with "U_E" (vertical) and "t" (horizontal) and a sinusoidal curve; on the right a sketch of axes with "I_B" (vertical) and "t" (horizontal) and a slightly wavy curve.
