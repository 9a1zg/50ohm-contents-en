2) Detailed description: The graphic consists of three rows one below the other with two diagrams side by side in each. The diagrams on the left are all identical and have a horizontal axis "f" and a vertical axis "Power". The horizontal axis has scale marks at "300 Hz" and "3 kHz". At 300 Hz there is a longer vertical line and at 3 kHz a shorter vertical line. Their upper ends are joined by a line sloping down to the right. The diagrams on the right have a horizontal axis "f" and a vertical axis without a label. The horizontal axis has a scale mark "f_T" in each case. In the first row there is a vertical line at f_T; to the left and right of it, standing on the baseline, mirror-image quadrilaterals with top edges sloping down to the left and to the right are shown. Above them is a red double-headed arrow with the label "6 kHz". In the second row a quadrilateral stands on the baseline to the right of f_T with a top edge sloping down to the right, above it a red double-headed arrow with the label "2.7 kHz". In the third row a quadrilateral stands on the baseline to the left of f_T with a top edge sloping down to the left, above it a red double-headed arrow with the label "2.7 kHz". Between the diagrams of each row there is a blue arrow pointing to the right, labelled "AM" (1st row), "USB" (2nd row) and "LSB" (3rd row) respectively.
