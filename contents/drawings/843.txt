Brief summary: Block diagram of a transverter with antenna, RX/TX changeover switches, RF amplifier, RX mixer and TX mixer, a 38.666 MHz generator with a multiplier to 116 MHz, driver, PA and a TRX for 28–30 MHz; arrows show the signal flow and text refers to 144/146 MHz.

Detailed description: On the left is an antenna symbol, which is coupled to a horizontal line via a two-position switch labelled "RX" and "TX". In the receive direction the line leads to the right to a triangle block labelled "RF" ("HF") (amplifier), whose output is connected to a square block "RX mixer" ("RX‑Mischer") (square with crossed circle). In the middle at the bottom is a block labelled "38.666 MHz"; in it a "G" and wavy lines are drawn. To the right of it an arrow points to a square block with a diagonal dividing line and the digits "1" (top) and "3" (bottom). From this block a vertical line labelled "116 MHz" leads up to the "RX mixer" (arrow upwards) and down to the "TX mixer" ("TX‑Mischer") (arrow downwards). At the bottom left is a square block "TX mixer" (also with a crossed circle). Its output leads to the left to a triangle block "Driver" ("Treiber") and on to the left to a rectangular block "PA"; from there the line goes back towards the antenna switch. On the right of the picture is a small rectangular block "TRX" with the label "28–30 MHz" below it; beside it is a further two-position switch labelled "RX" and "TX", which connects the TRX to the rest of the circuit. At the bottom right is the text "TX: 28 MHz + 116 MHz = 144 MHz" and "30 MHz + 116 MHz = 146 MHz". All connections are shown with arrows in the direction of signal flow.
