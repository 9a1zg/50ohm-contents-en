1) Short description: Graphic with six rows; on the left of each a smartphone or a laptop, on the right a radio; in between, different kinds of connection: radio waves, network, audio with CAT interface, audio with data mode interface with and without CAT, and directly by USB.

2) Detailed description: The graphic consists of six rows one below the other and shows different kinds of connection between a smartphone or laptop and a radio. In each row a smartphone (at the top) or a laptop is shown on the left. On the right there is a radio in each case. In detail, the following connections are shown:
Row 1: on the left a smartphone with radio waves pointing to the right, on the right a radio with radio waves pointing to the left; the two devices connected to each other by radio waves
Row 2: on the left a laptop, on the right a radio; the two devices connected to each other via a "Network"
Row 3: on the left a laptop with an "Audio" and a "USB" connection, on the right a radio with an "Audio" and a "CAT" connection; the two devices connected to each other directly via the audio connections and, via the USB and CAT connections, through a "CAT interface"
Row 4: on the left a laptop with a "USB" connection, on the right a radio with an "Audio" and a "PTT" connection; the two devices connected to each other via a "Data mode interface"
Row 5: on the left a laptop with a "USB" connection, on the right a radio with an "Audio", a "CAT" and a "PTT" connection; the two devices connected to each other via a "Data mode" interface
Row 6: on the left a laptop with a "USB" connection, on the right a radio with a "USB" connection; the two devices connected to each other directly
