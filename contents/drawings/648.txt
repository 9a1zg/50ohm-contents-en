1) Short description: Schematic drawing of a mountain with an antenna on the summit; on the left and on the right a person stands with a small device with a whip antenna, from which orange wavy lines emanate.

2) Detailed description: Along the bottom edge runs a thin horizontal ground line. In the middle rises a large mountain with two summits of different heights. On the higher summit stands a short vertical mast; from its top two short rods branch off diagonally up to the left and up to the right (antenna). On the left at the foot of the mountain stands a simple stick figure holding in its hand a small rectangular device with a short whip antenna pointing diagonally upwards; from the device several concentric, orange, arc-shaped lines emanate, pointing up to the right. As a mirror image of this, on the right at the foot of the mountain stands a second stick figure with an identical device and antenna; in front of this device too several concentric, orange, arc-shaped lines can be seen, pointing up to the left.
