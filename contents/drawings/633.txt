2) Detailed description: The figure shows how a dipole is coupled to a coaxial cable. The inner conductor is connected to "Dipole leg 1" ("Dipolschenkel 1") (left) and the outer conductor to "Dipole leg 2" ("Dipolschenkel 2") (right). Also shown are the currents on the outside of the inner conductor ("I_1"), on the inside of the outer conductor ("I_2") and on the outside of the outer conductor ("I_3"). I_1 flows at the top in dipole leg 1 and I_2 in dipole leg 2. I_3 flows down along the outer conductor to chassis. The relationship between the currents is given by an equation: "I_2 = I_1 – I_3". At the lower end of the coaxial cable the outer conductor and the inner conductor are connected to each other through a voltmeter with an AC symbol, and the outer conductor is earthed.
