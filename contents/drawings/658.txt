1) Short description: World map with three regions marked in colour and the national borders drawn in; at the bottom is a legend with the labels "Region 2", "Region 1" and "Region 3" (from left to right).

2) Detailed description: The map shows the continents as coloured areas on a white background; the oceans are white, national borders are drawn as thin white lines. America (North, Central and South America) and adjacent islands are coloured green. Europe, Africa and the western part of Central Asia are coloured orange. The eastern and southern parts of Asia as well as Australia, New Zealand and the islands in the Pacific are coloured yellow. At the bottom edge of the map a narrow strip with text labelling can be seen: on the left "Region 2" in green, in the middle "Region 1" in orange and on the right "Region 3" in yellow.
