1) Short description: Coordinate diagram of U against t with a periodic sawtooth curve; the instants T, 2T and 3T are marked on the t‑axis.

2) Detailed description: On the left is a vertical axis with an arrow pointing upwards and the label "U", at the bottom a horizontal axis with an arrow pointing to the right and the label "t". Between 0 and T the curve rises in a straight line; shortly before T it falls vertically to a low value. The same pattern repeats between T and 2T and between 2T and 3T: each time a linear rise followed by a vertical fall. On the time axis the marks "T", "2T" and "3T" are entered level with the respective points where the curve falls. Before t=0 the curve is indicated as a dashed, rising section; after 3T a dashed, rising continuation is likewise drawn.
