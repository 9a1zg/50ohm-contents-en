1) Short description: Long vertical line embedded at its upper end in an upside-down isosceles triangle.

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol consists of a continuous vertical line. In the upper part of the line a large, upside-down isosceles triangle can be seen, with a horizontal line at the top and two lines running diagonally downwards, which meet at a point on the vertical line. There are no axes, scales, text or numerical labels and no further component symbols.
