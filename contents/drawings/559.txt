1) Short description: Circuit symbol for a transistor with a circle, one connection on the left ("B") and two connections on the right ("C" and "E"). An arrow labelled "U_BE" leads from "B" to "E".

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol for a transistor consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection "B" leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line. From this line two lines go to the right. The upper one runs diagonally up to the right and ends at a vertical connection "C". The lower one runs diagonally down to the right. At its end it has a filled triangular arrow, whose tip points to a vertical connection "E". From here a vertical line leads down and ends in a short horizontal line. Below the circuit symbol a slanting arrow leads from "B" to "E"; it is labelled "U_BE".
