1) Short description: Circuit symbol of a transistor with a circle, one connection on the left and two connections on the right.

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol for a transistor consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection labelled "2" leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line. From this line two lines go to the right. The upper one runs diagonally up to the right and ends at a short vertical connection, labelled "1". The lower one runs diagonally down to the right. At its end it has a filled triangular arrow, whose tip points to a short vertical connection. This connection is labelled "3". There are no further text labels.
