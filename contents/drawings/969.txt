Brief summary: Block diagram of a multi-stage receiver with an antenna, filters and three mixers, labelled with the frequencies 3–30 MHz, 50 MHz, 9 MHz and 455 kHz.

Detailed description: On the left is an antenna symbol, connected by an arrow to the right to a square with a filter symbol (wavy lines) and the label "RF" ("HF"); next to it/above it is "3–30 MHz". Next comes an arrow to a square with a circle in which an "X" is drawn (mixer). From below, an arrow points up onto this mixer from a separate box labelled "VFO"; in the VFO box there are three wavy lines and a small arrow pointing diagonally up to the right. After that an arrow leads to another filter square with the label "1st IF" ("1.ZF"); above it is "50 MHz". Next comes an arrow to a second mixer square (circle with "X"), which is fed from below by an arrow from a box "XO1"; in "XO1" there is the letter "G" at the top left, next to it a filter symbol (wavy lines) and at the bottom two horizontal lines. After that comes a filter square "2nd IF" ("2.ZF") with the label "9 MHz" above it. Next comes a third mixer (circle with "X"), fed from below from a box "XO2" with the same internal symbols ("G", wavy lines, two horizontal lines). Then there is another filter square "3rd IF" ("3.ZF") with the frequency "455 kHz" above it. On the far right an arrow points outwards. All connecting arrows run from left to right between the blocks.
