Brief summary: Line chart with several rising straight lines showing the basic attenuation of various coaxial cables per 100 m of cable length as a function of frequency.

Detailed description: Rectangular graphic with a dense grid. At the bottom is the x‑axis "Frequency [MHz]" ("Frequenz [MHz]"); the tick marks (from left to right) are labelled 1, 3.5, 5, 7, 10, 14, 20, 29, 50, 100, 145, 200, 300, 435, 1296, 2350, 4000, 5700. At the top the same frequency values are shown again. On the left is the y‑axis "Basic attenuation α0 per 100 m cable length in dB" ("Grunddämpfung α0 je 100 m Leitungslänge in dB"). At the visible edge the scale runs from 0.5 at the bottom to 300 at the top; in between, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100 and 200, among others, are marked (the right-hand side of the axis shows the same values). Several black, straight lines rising to the upper right are labelled with cable/dielectric details; from top to bottom (at high attenuation) they read: "PTFE 1.8 mm RG178", "Solid PE 2.9 mm RG174" ("Voll‑PE 2,9 mm RG174"), "Solid PE 4.95 mm RG58" ("Voll‑PE 4,95 mm RG58"), "Solid PE 10.3 mm RG213" ("Voll‑PE 10,3 mm RG213"). Below them runs a bundle of further lines labelled "Foam PE …" ("PE‑Schaum …") with various diameters, including "Foam PE 7.3 mm" ("PE‑Schaum 7,3 mm"), "Foam PE 4.9 mm" ("PE‑Schaum 4,9 mm"), "Foam PE 2.7 mm" ("PE‑Schaum 2,7 mm"), "Foam PE 1.64 mm" ("PE‑Schaum 1,64 mm") and "Foam PE solid screen" ("PE‑Schaum Massivschirm"). All the lines rise with increasing frequency; at the same frequency the foam PE lines lie lower in the chart than the solid PE and PTFE lines.
