1) Short description: Schematic representation of a coaxial cable whose outer conductor is connected to a horizontal radial and whose inner conductor is connected to a vertical radiator.

2) Detailed description: In the left-hand part of the representation there is a horizontal coaxial cable whose outer conductor is connected to a horizontal radial, which extends to the right-hand edge of the picture. The inner conductor of the coaxial cable first leads out of the cable to the right and is connected to the base of a vertical radiator. The coaxial cable, radial and radiator are labelled accordingly.
