1) Short description: Schematic representation of a resistor with four colour bands, which are labelled "1st digit", "2nd digit", "Multiplier" and "Tolerance".

2) Detailed description: The representation contains a beige resistor with a grey lead on the left and on the right. On the resistor four narrow colour bands can be seen from left to right: yellow, violet, orange and grey; between the bands the normal body colour is visible. Three black leader lines lead from below to the first three bands and are labelled "1st digit" (under the yellow band), "2nd digit" (under the violet band) and "Multiplier" (under the orange band). A further black leader line leads from above to the grey band and is labelled "Tolerance".
