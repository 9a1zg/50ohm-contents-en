) Detailed description: The figure shows the schematic diagram of a vertical radiator with a capacitor (two horizontal strokes) inserted at its lower end. The radiator is connected to the inner conductor of a coaxial cable leading downwards. From the outer conductor of the coaxial cable four horizontal conductors (radials) go outwards at intervals of 90°. Parallel to the vertical radiator there is an arrow pointing up and down, labelled "mech. > λ/4". Parallel to one of the radials an arrow pointing inwards and outwards can be seen, labelled "λ/4".
