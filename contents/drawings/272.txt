1) Short description: Circuit symbol with a circle, one connection on the left and two connections on the right, and an arrow pointing to the centre.

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection leads horizontally from outside into the circle and meets this vertical line in its lower part. Parallel to this line there are three small vertical strokes, from each of which a parallel horizontal line leads to the right. The upper one ends at a short vertical connection upwards; the middle one has an arrow pointing to the left, towards the vertical line, and is connected to the lower horizontal line inside the circle. The lower horizontal line has a short vertical connection downwards.
