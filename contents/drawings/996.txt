"Graph with the x-axis "Distance in kilometres" ("Distanz in kilo meter") from 0 to 600 and the y-axis "Height in kilometres" ("Höhe in kilo meter") from 0 to 400. Four curves are shown: two solid lines (day) and two dashed lines (night). The solid lines show lower paths with a short distance; the dashed lines rise higher and form arc-shaped curves with a long distance. In the legend at the top right the lines are identified as "Winter day" ("Winter Tag") (blue, solid), "Winter night" ("Winter Nacht") (blue, dashed), "Summer day" ("Sommer Tag") (red, solid) and "Summer night" ("Sommer Nacht") (red, dashed)."
