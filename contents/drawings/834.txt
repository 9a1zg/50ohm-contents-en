Brief summary: Diagram of an orange sinusoidal voltage U against time t with the markings T, Û, U_eff and U_ss.

Detailed description:
- On the left a vertical axis with an arrow pointing upwards and the label U; a horizontal axis with an arrow pointing to the right and the label t runs through the picture. On the t-axis there are several small, evenly spaced tick marks.
- At the top, above the graphic, a horizontal double-headed arrow across the whole width, labelled T.
- An orange sine curve begins on the left at U=0, rises to a positive peak to the left of the middle, falls through U=0 to the right of the middle, reaches a negative minimum further to the right and returns to U=0 at the right-hand edge (one full period).
- At the positive peak there is a vertical double-headed arrow from the level of the zero line up to the height of the peak, labelled Û; at the height of the peak runs a fine grey horizontal guide line.
- Further to the right, on the falling slope, a shorter vertical double-headed arrow points from the level of the zero line to the curve, labelled U_eff; its height is indicated by a short grey horizontal guide line.
- To the right of the middle a vertical line is drawn through the t-axis; on it a long vertical double-headed arrow with heads pointing up and down, labelled U_ss, whose upper and lower heads mark approximately the height of the positive peak and the depth of the negative trough respectively.
