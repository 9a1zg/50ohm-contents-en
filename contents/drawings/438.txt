Brief summary: Block diagram of a digital signal path with the blocks "Clock generator" ("Taktgenerator"), "Address counter" ("Adresszähler"), "Sine table" ("Sinus‑Tabelle"), "Register" and "D/A converter" ("D/A Umsetzer"), with arrows from left to right, an upper feedback path and the label "f_out" on the right.

Detailed description: On the left is a rectangle with a square-wave symbol (several clock edges); to the left of it the text "Clock generator" is set vertically. From the right-hand edge of this block a horizontal line leads to the right; shortly after it there is a small, filled node. At this node a thin line starts at the top, leads upwards, extends to the right and is later brought back down again (upper feedback path). On the main line follows a rectangle labelled "+1"; vertically beside it is "Address counter". After that, on the connection, lies a slanting stroke "/" as a bus marking; above it is "N", below it, vertically, "Bits". Then follows a rectangle with a small coordinate-axes symbol and a sine curve with arrows; beside it, vertically, the label "Sine table". This is followed by a narrow rectangle with a triangle symbol pointing to the right. After that comes a further rectangle with the vertical label "Register". Then follows a rectangle with a diagonal dividing line; in the two triangular areas are the letters "D" (top) and "A" (bottom); vertically beside it is "D/A converter". From there an arrow leads to the right to the label "f_out", set in italics. All connections are drawn as arrows in the direction of flow from left to right.
