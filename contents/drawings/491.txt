1) Summary: Circuit diagram with two transistors arranged one above the other between an upper (+) and a lower (−) supply rail, a transformer on each of the left and the right, and an inductor, two capacitors and two resistors.

2) Detailed description: At the top runs a horizontal line with a terminal "+" at the far right; at the bottom a horizontal line with a terminal "−" at the far right, and an earth symbol is connected in the middle of the lower line. From the upper "+" bus a vertical coil (inductor) leads down on the right to a node. At this node lie the upper connection of a winding drawn to the left of it (part of the right-hand transformer) and the upper plate of a capacitor; the lower plate of this capacitor goes down to a horizontal line, which is taken further to the left to the central node between the two transistors and to the right to the lower connection of the said winding; this line continues further down to the "−" rail. To the right of the pair of windings just described stands a second winding (right-hand transformer) with two open terminals to the right. In the middle of the diagram there are two bipolar transistors drawn in circles, one above the other; their bases are taken to the left. The common central connection between the two transistors is a node that leads to the left to a vertical common line and to the right (as described above) to the capacitor and transformer branch. The upper connection of the upper transistor is connected to the "+" bus via the left-hand vertical line; the lower connection of the lower transistor is connected to the "−" rail. On the left a further transformer is drawn: a right-hand winding with three connection points (upper end, centre tap with a dot marking, lower end) and a left-hand winding with two open terminals to the left. The centre tap of the right-hand winding is connected to the left-hand vertical common line; on this line there is a resistor at the top to the "+" bus and a resistor at the bottom to the "−" rail. The upper and lower ends of the right-hand winding each lead a short way to the right to the bases of the two transistors. At top left, in addition, a capacitor is connected at the side to the upper section of the left-hand vertical line. Connection points are marked as black dots.
