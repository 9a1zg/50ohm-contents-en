1) Short description: Semicircular graphic with a transmitter on the left and a receiver on the right; a yellow and a pink layer above the earth marked in blue; elliptical figure labelled "Sporadic-E" in the yellow layer; two dashed lines that lead outwards through the layers, and a continuous line that leads from the transmitter to the elliptical figure and on to the receiver.

2) Detailed description: The semicircular graphic shows the position of the ionosphere, with two concentrically arranged, arc-shaped layers above the earth, which is drawn in blue at the bottom, and the path of VHF signals. On the earth, on the left and on the right, stands a small black antenna symbol, labelled "Transmitter" (left) and "Receiver" (right). From the transmitter two dashed red lines lead at different angles first through a narrow yellow layer and then through a broad pink layer, and end at the top edge of the picture. A continuous red line leads from the transmitter up to an elliptical figure in the yellow layer and down to the receiver. The elliptical figure is labelled "Sporadic-E".
