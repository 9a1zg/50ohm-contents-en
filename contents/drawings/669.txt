1) Short description: Schematic representation of a coaxial cable whose outer conductor is connected to chassis and whose inner conductor is connected to a vertical radiator.

2) Detailed description: In the left-hand part of the representation there is a horizontal coaxial cable whose outer conductor is connected to chassis. The inner conductor of the coaxial cable first leads out of the cable to the right and is connected to the base of a vertical radiator. The coaxial cable and radiator are labelled accordingly.
