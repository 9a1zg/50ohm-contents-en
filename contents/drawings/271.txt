1) Short description: Two circuit symbols ("1" and "2"), each with a circle, one connection on the left and two connections on the right; the left-hand connection of the left symbol with an arrow pointing right, that of the right symbol with an arrow pointing left.

2) Detailed description: Two circuit symbols ("1" and "2") consist of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line in its lower part. In the left-hand symbol this connection has an arrow pointing towards the line; in the right-hand symbol the arrow points away from the line. From the vertical line two parallel horizontal lines go to the right inside the circle. The upper one ends at a short vertical connection upwards, the lower one at a short vertical connection downwards.
