Detailed description: A Cartesian coordinate system with arrowheads pointing upwards and to the right; the vertical axis is labelled "U" and the horizontal one "t". There are no tick marks or numbers. A thin blue line shows a dense sequence of narrow, sine-like half-waves, which always run above the t-axis and return to the axis between the peaks. The spacing of the half-waves is almost constant, while their height varies slowly: on the left there are arcs of medium height to begin with, followed by higher ones; then the height gradually decreases down to several small arcs at about the middle, after which the height grows markedly again to several large arcs on the right, before it decreases slightly at the very edge. At no point does the curve go below the t-axis.
