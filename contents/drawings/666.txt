1) Short description: Upper part: horizontal line with a circuit symbol for a diode in the middle; coming from the left an arrow "Anode", leaving to the right an arrow "Cathode" ("Kathode"). Lower part: horizontal line; two red lines arranged as mirror images about the line, coming from the upper left and the lower left, which meet on the horizontal line at an angle; vertical red line through the two red lines, forming an "A" lying on its side; below, at the foot of the "A", the letters "node"; at the apex of the angle a vertical blue line; at its intersection with the horizontal line two blue lines leaving up to the right and down to the right, forming a large "K"; below, at the foot of the "K", the letters "athode".

2) Detailed description: The figure consists of two parts. In the upper part there is a horizontal line with a circuit symbol for a diode in the middle (tip of the triangle pointing to the right). On the line, to the left of the symbol, an arrow pointing to the right is drawn, labelled "Anode". To the right of the symbol is an arrow pointing to the right, labelled "Cathode" ("Kathode"). In the lower part a horizontal line is drawn. From the upper left and from the lower left there are two red lines arranged as mirror images, which meet on the horizontal line at an angle. Through the middle of the two red lines runs a vertical red line, forming an "A" lying on its side. At the foot of the "A", downwards, in small lettering, are the letters "node". At the apex of the angle of the red line there is a vertical blue line, from whose intersection with the horizontal line two blue lines leave up to the right and down to the right, forming a large "K". At the foot of the "K", to the right, in small lettering, are the letters "athode".
