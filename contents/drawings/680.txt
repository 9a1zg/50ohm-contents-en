2) Detailed description: Shown is a block diagram of several modules connected by two horizontal lines. At the far left is a power source, shown with two terminals and the label "~ 230 V" as well as, in grey italics, "High voltage" ("Hohe Spannung"). To the right follows a power supply unit ("⎓ 13.8 V"). Its upper output is marked with a red "+" and its lower output with a black "–". From the upper output a red line runs via a fuse drawn in red to the upper input of a transceiver (TRX). The line contains a red arrow pointing to the right and is labelled in red "Direct current (I)" ("Gleichstrom (I)"). From the lower output of the power supply unit a black line runs directly to the lower input of the transceiver. The transceiver is connected to chassis. To the right of the transceiver there is an output leading to an antenna. Between the power supply unit and the transceiver, in grey italics, is "Risk of short circuit and reverse polarity" ("Kurzschluss- und Verpolungsgefahr").
