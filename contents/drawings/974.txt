1) Brief summary: Block diagram of a radio receiving path with an antenna, "1st filter" (430–434 MHz), block "RF", "Mixer", "2nd filter" (28–30 MHz) and output "RX", supplemented by a lower path with "XO" and a block "f/9f", which leads via "fosz" to the mixer.

2) Detailed description: On the left is an antenna symbol whose line leads to the right into a rectangular block "1st filter" with three curved lines; below it is "430–434 MHz". From there an arrow points to the right to a rectangular block "RF", which contains a triangle symbol (amplifier symbol). Another arrow leads to the right into a rectangular block "Mixer", shown as a circle with a diagonal cross (multiplication symbol). From the mixer an arrow goes to the right to the rectangular block "2nd filter" with three curved lines; below it is "28–30 MHz". To the right of it is the label "RX" next to a small open terminal circle. Below this upper signal path is another path: a rectangular block "XO" with three curved lines is on the left, and an arrow leads from "XO" to the right to a block with a diagonal dividing line and the labels "f" (bottom left in the block) and "9f" (top right in the block). From this block an arrow leads upwards to the "Mixer"; next to this arrow is "fosz". Below the "XO" block two short lines lead down to two on/off toggle switches (each with a small open contact point at the top), below each of which a crystal symbol (rectangular case with a narrow centre line) is drawn. All connections are shown with thin lines; the signal function is indicated by arrowheads in the direction of flow (left to right in the upper path, at the bottom from left to right and then upwards).
