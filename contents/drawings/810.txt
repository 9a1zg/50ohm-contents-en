Brief summary: Block diagram of a receiver with an antenna, three amplifier stages, two mixers, a product detector, an AF stage and an AGC line feeding back to the amplifiers.

Detailed description: At the far left is an antenna symbol, which leads via a black line with an arrow to the right into a blue block with a triangle; below it is "RF" ("HF"). This is followed by a red block with a circle and a cross; above it is "1st mixer" ("1. Mischer"). Below this block a vertical line leads down to a small square with a symbol (oblique arrow pointing up to the right above a wavy line). To the right a black arrowed line goes into a blue block with a triangle; below it is "1st IF" ("1.ZF"). After that follows a further red block with a circle and a cross; above it is "2nd mixer" ("2. Mischer"). Below this block a vertical line goes to a small square with the inscription "G" and three lines below it. Then follows a blue block with a triangle; below it is "2nd IF" ("2.ZF"). Next comes a red block with a circle and a cross; above it is "Product detector" ("Produktdetektor"). Below it a vertical line leads to a small square with a wavy-line symbol. To the right the black arrowed line leads into a blue block with a triangle; below it is "AF" ("NF"). At the right-hand edge the line ends in a loudspeaker symbol. Above the chain runs a yellow line with the label "AGC", from which three yellow arrows pointing downwards point into the blue blocks "RF", "1st IF" and "2nd IF". The amplifier blocks are blue, the mixer and product detector blocks red, the AGC line yellow; the direction of the signal is marked by arrows from left to right.
