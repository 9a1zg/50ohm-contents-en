1) Short description: Grid graphic with twelve circuit symbols in three rows (battery, resistor, capacitor, inductor; diode, LED, switch, transistor; antenna, chassis and earth, voltmeter, ammeter), each with an English label below it.

2) Detailed description: The graphic is arranged in three rows of four circuit symbols each. Below each symbol is a label. Upper row from left to right: "Battery": two vertical lines, of which the left one is thinner than the right one; "Resistor": horizontal rectangular block; "Capacitor": two vertical lines of equal length; "Inductor": four semicircles pointing upwards. Middle row: "Diode": triangle pointing to the right on a baseline; "LED": triangle pointing to the right with two small arrows pointing diagonally up to the right away from the triangle; "Switch": two unconnected terminals, from the left one a line pointing up to the right; "Transistor": circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle, with a horizontal line from the left and two lines to the upper right and lower right, the latter with a triangular arrow pointing outwards. Lower row: "Antenna": vertical line with a V-shaped symbol at the top end; "Chassis and earth": the one symbol with a short vertical line meeting a horizontal line below it, and the other symbol with a short vertical line above three horizontal lines that become shorter towards the bottom; "Voltmeter": circle with the letter "V" in the middle; "Ammeter": circle with the letter "A" in the middle.
