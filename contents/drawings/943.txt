Detailed description: A Cartesian coordinate system shows a horizontal axis with an arrow to the right and the label "t" and a vertical axis with an arrow upwards; there are short unlabelled tick marks, but no numerical values. At the top right is a legend: a short green line with "P", an orange line with "I" and a blue line with "U". Three smooth sine curves run across the width: the blue curve has the largest amplitude and the longest wavelength; it starts on the left near zero and rises. The orange curve has a somewhat smaller amplitude and a similar wavelength to the blue one, but is phase-shifted; on the left it is above the zero line and falls while the blue one rises. The green curve has the smallest amplitude and a shorter wavelength (it oscillates more often than the blue and orange curves); the area between the green curve and the horizontal zero line is shaded semi-transparent green, both above and below the zero line, according to the sign of the curve.
