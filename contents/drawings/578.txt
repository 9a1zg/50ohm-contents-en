1) Short description: Control and display panel of a radio with a large frequency display "144.315.0", several bar graphs with scales and a spectrum and waterfall display; elements are marked ① to ④.

2) Detailed description: At top left are large seven-segment digits "144.315.0", to the right of them "USB" and below that "TX". At top right is a loudspeaker symbol above a horizontal bar with the label "0" on the left and "100" on the right; the bar is about four-fifths filled with dark grey. Below on the left there is a long horizontal bar with a scale. Above it are the values "1 3 5 7 9 +20 +40", below the bar is a second scale "1 5 10 15 20". The bar is filled with dark grey from the left up to the mark "9"; the rest is white. On the left of the lower scale bar an arrow with the circled marker "②" points to the bar. To the right of it is a further horizontal bar with the marks "1 2 3 ∞". This bar is filled with dark grey from the left up to a value of approx. 1.3; the rest is white. An arrow with the circled marker "①" points to the bar. A horizontal dividing line runs through the middle of the figure. In the field below it, in the middle, a spectrum with a grey background and a black curve can be seen: left of centre the curve rises steeply to a peak and then falls away irregularly. On the right in the middle field is the circled marker "③". In the lower field a waterfall display appears: dark background, in the middle a very bright, narrow vertical strip; to the left of it a narrow, vertically dashed line; to the right of it a vertically arranged pattern of lines. At bottom left is the circled marker "④".
