1) Short description: Semicircular graphic with a transmitter on the left and a receiver on the right, showing a pink layer above the earth marked in blue as well as a green "Ground wave" and a red "Sky wave". Between the end of the ground wave and the point where the sky wave first comes down, a "Skip zone" is drawn along the earth's surface.

2) Detailed description: The semicircular graphic shows the position of the ionosphere above the earth, which is drawn in blue at the bottom. On the earth, on the left and on the right, there is a small black antenna symbol, labelled "Transmitter" (left) and "Receiver" (right). From the transmitter a short, straight green line points towards the middle, labelled "Ground wave". A red line, labelled "Sky wave", starts near the transmitter, rises steeply, meets a pink, arched layer in the upper part of the picture, runs obliquely down to the middle and touches the surface of the earth there, then rises obliquely again to the pink layer and afterwards falls obliquely towards the receiver. The pink layer extends broadly across the whole graphic and is unlabelled. Between the end of the ground wave and the point where the sky wave first comes down, a "Skip zone" is drawn in the form of an arc along the earth's surface.
