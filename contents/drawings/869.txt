Short description: Spectral diagram with fundamental frequency f0 and three overtones (2nd, 3rd and 4th harmonics) as vertical lines along the frequency axis.

Detailed description: A coordinate system shows the amplitude A (arrow upwards) against the frequency f (arrow to the right). On the baseline stand four vertical lines: on the left a black line at the label "f0" (italic) with "1 Harm." (1st harmonic) below it; to the right of it three yellow lines with the labels "1. OW 2 Harm.", "2. OW 3 Harm." and "3. OW 4 Harm." (1st to 3rd overtone, 2nd to 4th harmonic). The yellow lines are shorter than the black one and differ in height. Above the yellow lines grey dotted vertical guide lines run upwards. Along the top edge of the picture three horizontal grey double-headed arrows each mark the spacing "f0" between the lines. Between the second and third yellow lines is the text "Harmonics (OW)" ("Oberwellen (OW)") in orange.
