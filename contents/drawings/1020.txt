- General: Each panel shows black lines with filled nodes (black dots) and terminal circles drawn open; the labels "U_E" (left) and "U_A" (right) are in blue, each next to an arrow pointing downwards. The digits "1", "2" and "3" are each in a circle near the upper right area of the panels.

To the left of the stack of components is the blue arrow "U_E" pointing downwards, to the right of it the blue arrow "U_A" pointing downwards.
- Panel 2: A vertically drawn resistor "R1" connects an upper line to a middle node. On the left is the blue, downward-pointing arrow "U_E", on the right the blue, downward-pointing arrow "U_A".
- Panel 3: A horizontally drawn resistor "R1" runs from the left to a central node. From there a short line goes to the right to an open terminal circle, and a vertical line goes down to a capacitor "C1", which is connected to the lower line. On the right, at the end of the lower line, is a further open terminal circle. On the left is the blue arrow "U_E" pointing downwards, on the right the blue arrow "U_A" pointing downwards.
