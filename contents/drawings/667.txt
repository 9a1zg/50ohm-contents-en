1) Short description: Perspective representation of a closed pipe circuit with a cylindrical tank on the left, an open stopcock at the top and a unit with a paddle wheel on the right; water flow coloured blue, beginning above the paddle wheel, running over the paddle wheel and ending at the bottom, to the left of it, in the pipe system.

2) Detailed description: On the left stands a large cylindrical tank with a domed lid and a bolted cover on a base plate. Several pipe connections branch off from its side, among them a lateral flange-like connection at the bottom and a block with several valve housings and screw fittings. From the tank a pipe circuit leads via an open stopcock at the top to a second unit. This unit consists of a paddle wheel with many radially arranged blades. A water flow coloured blue runs from above the paddle wheel over the paddle wheel and ends at the bottom in the pipe system.
