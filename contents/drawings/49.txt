2) Detailed description: On the left a large cylindrical roll of layers lying one on top of another is shown, rolled up in a spiral. The roll is labelled "Roll". The outer, unrolled end of the roll points to the right. On it two sheets labelled "Metal foils" can be seen, each of them applied to layers that project beyond these sheets. These sheets projecting at the sides are labelled "Dielectric". The inner layer, with the dielectric and the metal foil applied to it, is marked with a jagged line at the end of the layer so that the outer layer can be seen.
