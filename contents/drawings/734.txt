1) Short description: Schematic representation of layers of air lying one above the other, with the labels "Cold air" ("Kalte Luft") (at the bottom, with a blue wavy line), "Warm air" ("Warme Luft") and "Very cold air" ("Sehr kalte Luft") above a city skyline; four white arrows from the very cold air into the warm air.

2) Detailed description: The graphic shows a black city skyline with buildings and towers of different heights against a light blue background. Above it are several layers of air with the labels "Cold air" ("Kalte Luft") (with a blue wavy line to the left and right of the label), above it "Warm air" ("Warme Luft") (in a yellowish layer that thins out towards the top) and above that "Very cold air" ("Sehr kalte Luft"). At the boundary between the warm air and the very cold air there are two horizontal white stripes. Four white, curved arrows point from the layer of very cold air into the lower part of the warm air.
