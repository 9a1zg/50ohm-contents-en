1) Short description: Control and display panel of a power supply unit with digital displays for voltage and current, a rotary knob for setting the voltage, on/off buttons, an overload indicator and coloured output sockets for the DC voltage.

2) Detailed description: At the top left is a rectangular seven-segment display with the label "V" above it; it shows "13.8". At the top right is an identical display with the label "A" above it; it shows "00.0". In the middle is the text "Voltage setting" ("Spannungseinstellung") above a large round rotary knob with a single marker line at the top. At the bottom left is a vertical double panel with two buttons, "ON" at the top, "OFF" at the bottom. On the right in the middle is a round yellow indicator LED with the label "Overload" ("Überlast"). Below it there are two round sockets with polarity signs: on the left blue with "−", on the right red with "+". Below the sockets is the text "Output" ("Ausgang").
