1) Short description: Perspective view of an isotropic radiator with spherical radiation, three black axes and a segment drawn in blue on the surface of the sphere.

2) Detailed description: The figure shows in perspective an isotropic radiator with spherical radiation. The radiator is drawn in the centre as a small red dot. Three black arrows go out from it: one arrow upwards, one arrow to the right and one arrow forwards towards the viewer. Around the centre lies a light grey spherical surface, which is represented by a perspective ellipse. To the left of the centre, in red lettering, is "Isotropic radiator". From the centre, between the arrow to the right and the arrow forwards, two thin grey lines run to the edge of the surface of the sphere. From the points where they meet the edge, two circular arcs go upwards along the surface of the sphere and meet at the upward arrow. Between the two lines, at half height, there is a quadrilateral area coloured blue, whose corners are joined to the centre by blue lines.
