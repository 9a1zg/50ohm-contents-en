Short: Circuit diagram with a vertical inductor (autotransformer) with a centre tap to M and earth, taken at the top to terminal A and at the bottom to terminal B; labels "50 Ω", "Zant" and twice "8".

Detail: At the top runs a horizontal conductor track with open terminals at both ends, labelled "A" on the left and on the right; in its middle sits a node, from which a line leads vertically downwards to the coil (inductor). The inductor is drawn vertically; at about its middle a tap is taken to the left, which runs to an open terminal "M"; on this horizontal line there is an earth symbol (chassis) pointing downwards. The lower end of the inductor is connected to a line to the right, which leads to an open terminal "B". To the right of the inductor is the label "Zant". Parallel to the inductor, a little to the right of it, runs a dashed vertical line; beside the inductor the number "8" is entered twice (once in the upper and once in the lower section). To the left of the upper conductor track is "50 Ω". Junctions are marked as black dots.
