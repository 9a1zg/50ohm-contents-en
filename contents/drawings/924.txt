Short description: Four circuit symbols of capacitors arranged side by side, labelled "a.", "b.", "c." and "d." at the top respectively; variants with a plus sign, a diagonal arrow marking and a diagonal line with a short crossbar are shown.

Detailed description:
- a.: Above the symbol is "a.". A normal capacitor symbol with two short, parallel, horizontal plates; a continuous vertical connecting line runs through the middle of the symbol.
- b.: Above the symbol is "b.". As with a., but the lower plate is drawn as a wide filled black bar; at the right of the upper, thin plate is a small "+". A vertical connecting line runs through the middle.
- c.: Above the symbol is "c.". Two parallel horizontal plates with a central vertical connecting line; a diagonal line with an arrowhead runs from bottom left to top right across the plates.
- d.: Above the symbol is "d.". Two parallel horizontal plates with a central vertical connecting line; a diagonal line without an arrow crosses the plates and ends at the top right in a short crossbar at right angles to it.
