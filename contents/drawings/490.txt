Detailed description:
- Oscillogram: Rectangular grid with the vertical label "2 V/Div." on the left and "0 V" at bottom left. On it a thick, repeating sawtooth curve over several periods: it rises at a slant, reaches a rounded peak and then falls in a straight line back to the line at 0 V; the amplitude spans about one to two squares.
- Circuit diagram: Two horizontal lines, the upper one with terminal "A" on the right, the lower one with terminal "B" on the right; on the left of each an open solder tag. Connected to the upper line are (from left to right): a vertical resistor "470 Ω" down to a central node; in the middle a transistor symbol in a circle (three connections, an arrow at the emitter), whose upper connection goes to the upper line and whose lower connection leads to the same central node; on the right a vertical resistor "1 kΩ" from the upper to the lower line. From the central node a Zener diode leads down on the left to the lower line, labelled "5.6 V"; also from the central node a polarised capacitor leads down to the lower line, with "+" at the upper connection and labelled "47 µF". All the connections are marked with nodes (filled dots).
