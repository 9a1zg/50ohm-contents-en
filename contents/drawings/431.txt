1) Short description: Series circuit diagram with a conductor drawn in alternating directions, with six voltage sources arranged in the vertical parts of the conductor, between a "+" terminal at top left and a "−" terminal at top right.

2) Detailed description: From an open terminal labelled "+" at top left a vertical conductor leads downwards to a symbol for a voltage source, in which the longer crossbar lies above the shorter crossbar. The conductor then runs in alternating directions (horizontally to the right, vertically upwards, horizontally to the right, vertically downwards, and so on) through a total of five further identical symbols, so that all the voltage sources are connected in series. At the right-hand end the last vertical part of the conductor leads upwards to an open terminal labelled "−". There are no values or further labels.
