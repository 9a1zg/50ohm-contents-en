1) Short description: Perspective representation of the position of a satellite with "Azimuth" and "Elevation" in relation to a circular area with the angle markings "0° N", "90° E" ("90° O"), "180° S" and "270° W" and above it "90° zenith" ("90° Zenit").

2) Detailed description: In the middle of the figure there is the perspective representation of a circular area with the angle markings "0° N", "90° E" ("90° O"), "180° S" and "270° W" and above it "90° zenith" ("90° Zenit"). At the top right a satellite is drawn, whose position in space is determined by means of the "Elevation" (in red lettering with a red arrow for the vertical deviation from the circular area) and the "Azimuth" (in blue lettering with a blue arrow for the horizontal deviation from 0° N on the circular area).
