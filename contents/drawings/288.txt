1) Short description: Circuit symbol for a PNP transistor with a circle, one connection on the left ("+2.0 V") and two connections on the right ("–5.6 V" at the top and "+1.4 V" at the bottom).

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol for a PNP transistor consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection labelled "+2.0 V" (base) leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line. Two lines branch off from this line. The upper one runs diagonally up to the right and ends at a vertical connection labelled "–5.6 V" (collector). The lower one runs diagonally down to the right. At its end it has a filled triangular arrow whose tip points to the vertical line (emitter). This connection is labelled "+1.4 V".
