Short summary: Line chart "Attenuation [dB]" against "Time [hours]" with three curves (3.6 MHz, 7.1 MHz, 14.15 MHz), which stay mostly close to 0 dB over the day and dip downwards to differing degrees around midday, most strongly at 3.6 MHz.

Detailed description: Rectangular plot with a grid. The y-axis on the left is labelled "Attenuation [dB]" and shows values from 0 at the top downwards into negative dB, with major ticks at 0, −5, −10, −15, −20 (and below). The x-axis at the bottom is labelled "Time [hours]" and carries scale marks from 0 to 24 in steps of two. At the bottom left is a legend with three entries: an orange line "3.6 MHz", a yellow line "7.1 MHz" and a green-turquoise line "14.15 MHz". All three curves start at 0 dB, stay close to 0 dB until late morning and show dips at around 12–13 hours: the orange curve (3.6 MHz) falls the most, very narrowly, to below −20 dB with a sharp minimum shortly after 12 hours; the yellow curve (7.1 MHz) forms a likewise narrow dip to about −6 to −8 dB at the same time; the green-turquoise curve (14.15 MHz) shows only a shallow kink just below 0 dB. After the minimum all the curves rise again and return to 0 dB by about 16–17 hours. The plot has a black frame; the background is light, the grid lines are grey.
