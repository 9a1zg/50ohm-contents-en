1) Short description: Circuit symbol with a circle, one connection on the left and two connections on the right.

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line. Two lines go off to the right from this line. The upper one runs diagonally up to the right and ends at a short vertical connection. The lower one runs diagonally down to the right. At its end it has a filled triangular arrow, whose point is directed at a short vertical connection. There are no text or axis labels.
