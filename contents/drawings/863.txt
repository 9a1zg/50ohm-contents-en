A vertical line runs through the circle: at the top is "C" (grey), at the bottom "E" (grey). On the vertical line above the circle a downward-pointing orange arrow is labelled "I_C"; below the circle there is likewise a downward-pointing orange arrow with "I_E". To the right of the vertical line a blue downward-pointing arrow labelled "U_CE" runs from "C" towards "E". From the area at the bottom left beside "B" a blue arrow pointing diagonally down to the right, labelled "U_BE", points towards the emitter region. The labels "I_B", "I_C" and "I_E" are orange, "U_BE" and "U_CE" blue, the terminal letters "B", "C" and "E" grey.
