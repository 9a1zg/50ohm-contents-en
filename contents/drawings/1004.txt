Summary: Schematic drawing of a "Real voltmeter" ("Reales Spannungsmessgerät"), in which a voltmeter (circle with "V") and a resistor labelled "Ri" lie between two horizontal lines, marked by a blue-shaded area with a dashed outline.

Detailed description: Two horizontal black lines run from left to right; on the left each of them ends in an open terminal circle. On the right a light-blue shaded area with a dashed border can be seen, below it the label "Real voltmeter" ("Reales Spannungsmessgerät"). Inside this area, on the right, is a circular symbol with the letter "V", which connects the upper and the lower line. To the left of it a vertically oriented rectangular resistor symbol is drawn between the upper and the lower line; next to the symbol is "Ri". At the lower connection of the resistor a black arrow points downwards, next to it the label "I". The points where the resistor joins the lines are marked as black dots.
