1) Short description: Control and display panel of a radio with a large frequency display "144.315.0", several bar graphs with scales and a spectrum and waterfall display.

2) Detailed description: At top left are large seven-segment digits "144.315.0", to the right of them "USB" and below that "TX". At top right is a loudspeaker symbol above a horizontal bar with the label "0" on the left and "100" on the right; the bar is about four-fifths filled with dark grey. Below on the left there is a long horizontal bar with an S scale. Above it are the values "1 3 5 7 9 +20 +40", below the bar is a P scale with the values "1 5 10 15 20". The bar is filled with dark grey from the left up to the mark "7"; the rest is white. To the right of it is a horizontal "SWR" bar with the marks "1 2 3 ∞". This bar is coloured dark grey almost all the way. A horizontal dividing line runs through the middle of the figure. In the field below it, in the middle, a spectrum with a grey background and a black curve can be seen: left of centre the curve rises steeply to a peak and then falls away irregularly. In the lower field a waterfall display appears: dark background, in the middle a very bright, narrow vertical strip, which lies below the peak of the curve above it.
