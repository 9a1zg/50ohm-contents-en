1) Short description: Diagram of the electromagnetic spectrum with the amateur radio bands between "30 kHz" (bottom) and "300 MHz" (top); with three vertical scales: a scale for the names of the ranges "LW", "MF", "HF" and "VHF"; a scale for the frequency bands giving the upper and lower frequency in kHz or MHz; a scale for the wavelength in km or m from "2.2 km" (bottom) to "2 m" (top).

2) Detailed description: The diagram is arranged vertically and consists of three separate scales. The left-hand scale contains – read from the bottom – the names of the ranges "LW" (30 kHz–300 kHz), "MF" (300 kHz–3 MHz), "HF" (3 MHz–30 MHz) and "VHF" (30 MHz–300 MHz). In the middle is a scale with the amateur radio frequency bands: the upper frequencies are on the left, the lower frequencies on the right. The upper frequencies are shifted slightly upwards relative to the lower frequencies, depending on the width of the band.
