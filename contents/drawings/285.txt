1) Short description: Circuit symbol for an NPN transistor with a circle, one connection on the left ("+1.4 V") and two connections on the right ("+5.6 V" at the top and "+2.0 V" at the bottom).

2) Detailed description: The circuit symbol for an NPN transistor consists of a circle with a vertical line on the left inside the circle. From the left a connection labelled "+1.4 V" (base) leads horizontally into the circle and meets this vertical line. Two lines branch off from this line. The upper one runs diagonally up to the right and ends at a vertical connection labelled "+5.6 V" (collector). The lower one runs diagonally down to the right. At its end it has a filled triangular arrow whose tip points to a vertical connection labelled "+2.0 V" (emitter).
