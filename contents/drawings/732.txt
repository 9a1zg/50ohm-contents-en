1) Short description: Semicircular graphic with a transmitter on the left and a receiver on the right, showing a pink layer above the earth marked in blue, as well as a green "Ground wave" ("Bodenwelle") and a red "Sky wave" ("Raumwelle").

2) Detailed description: The semicircular graphic shows the position of the ionosphere above the earth, which is drawn in blue at the bottom. On the earth, on the left and on the right, stands a small black antenna symbol, labelled "Transmitter" ("Sender") (left) and "Receiver" ("Empfänger") (right). From the transmitter a short, straight green line points towards the middle, labelled "Ground wave" ("Bodenwelle"). A red line, labelled "Sky wave" ("Raumwelle"), begins near the transmitter, rises steeply, meets a pink, arc-shaped layer in the upper part of the picture, runs diagonally down to the middle and touches the surface of the earth there, then rises again diagonally up to the pink layer and then falls diagonally down towards the receiver. The pink layer extends broadly across the whole graphic and is unlabelled.
