Short description: Four circuit symbols for inductors in a 2×2 arrangement, labelled "Air-cored inductor", "Iron-cored inductor", "Ferrite-cored inductor" and "Variable inductor" respectively.

Detailed description: At the top left "Air-cored inductor" shows a horizontal conductor line with a central inductor winding of several semicircular arcs; to the left and right the conductor line continues straight. At the top right is "Iron-cored inductor" with the same winding, above which a solid rectangular bar is drawn as the core. At the bottom left "Ferrite-cored inductor" is shown: the same inductor, above it a dashed bar. At the bottom right "Variable inductor" shows an inductor winding in the conductor line, crossed by a diagonal arrow from bottom left to top right; the arrow has a black tip above the winding. All drawings are in black on a white background; the labels are directly beside the respective symbols.
