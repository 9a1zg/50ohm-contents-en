Detailed description: On the left is an antenna symbol, connected to a rectangle "1st filter" ("1. Filter") (with three wavy lines inside), next to it the frequency "436–440 MHz". An arrow leads to the right to a block "RF" ("HF") (triangle symbol). From there an arrow continues to the block "Mixer" ("Mischer") (circle with a diagonal cross). From the mixer an arrow on the right leads to the block "2nd filter" ("2. Filter") (again three wavy lines), with the label "28–30 MHz" below it. To the right of it a line leads to an open circle labelled "RX". Below runs a second path: a block "XO" (three wavy lines) is on the left; below it two single toggle switches are drawn, each leading to a crystal symbol (rectangular crystal resonator). From the "XO" block an arrow goes to the right to a rectangle with a diagonally divided field and the inscriptions "f" (top left) and "9f" (bottom right). From this block an upward line labelled "fosz" leads into the "Mixer". All connections are drawn as straight lines with arrowheads in the direction of signal flow; there are no axes.
