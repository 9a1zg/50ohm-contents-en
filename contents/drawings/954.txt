To the right of it follows a vertical coil (transformer) labelled "ü = 1 : 7"; to the left of this coil a dashed vertical line runs parallel. The upper terminal of the coil passes on the right into a horizontal thick wire; above it a double-headed arrow labelled "≈ λ_max / 2" indicates the length of this wire. The lower terminal of the coil is connected to a wire taken vertically downwards. To the right of it is a double-headed height arrow labelled "≥ 0.05 · λ_max".
