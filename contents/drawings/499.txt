Short description: Circuit diagram with a thick upper busbar, two middle resistors and two measuring branches built up at the sides with diodes, capacitors, adjustable resistors and one ammeter "A" each.

Detailed description: At the top runs a thickly drawn, horizontally curved busbar with rounded ends; at the outermost left and right ends there are small circle symbols with short connecting lines. From this upper rail four vertical lines lead down: on the left, twice in the middle, and on the right. In each of the two middle branches there is a rectangular resistor symbol (resistor), connected between the upper rail and a lower common line. On the left and on the right, a side branch is built up identically in each case: directly below the upper rail is a diode; below it is a node to which a capacitor (two parallel plates) is connected across the side conductor. Also from this node an adjustable resistor (rectangle with a diagonal arrow) leads vertically down to the lower common line. From the node between diode/capacitor/adjustable resistor a line leads in each case to a round meter symbol with the label "A" (ammeter). The lower connections of the two ammeters are connected to each other and to the lower common line via a dashed rectangular connecting line. Black dots mark the electrical nodes at crossing and branch points. There are no axes; the only text labels are the two "A"s in the meters.
