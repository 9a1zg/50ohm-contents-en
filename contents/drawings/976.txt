Brief summary: Block diagram of a receiver chain with an antenna, three mixer stages and three IF blocks, with the labels 3–30 MHz, 50 MHz, 9 MHz, 455 kHz and the oscillator boxes "VFO", "G 41 MHz" and "G 9.455 MHz".

Detailed description: From left to right: an antenna symbol leads via an arrow into a rectangle with a wave-shaped symbol; below it is "RF" ("HF"), above it (written vertically) "3–30 MHz". Next comes an arrow into a circle with an "X" (mixer symbol). From below an arrow points onto this circle from a smaller rectangle; in the small rectangle is a wave-shaped symbol with an arrow pointing diagonally upwards, below it the label "VFO". After that comes a rectangle with a wave-shaped symbol; below it "1st IF" ("1.ZF"), above it vertically "50 MHz". Next comes another circle with "X". From below an arrow points onto the circle from a small rectangle with "G", a wave-shaped symbol and three horizontal lines; below this small rectangle is "41 MHz". Then a rectangle with a wave-shaped symbol; below it "2nd IF" ("2.ZF"), above it vertically "9 MHz". Next comes a third circle with "X". From below an arrow points onto the circle from an identically designed small rectangle with "G", a wave-shaped symbol and three horizontal lines; below it is "9.455 MHz". Then a rectangle with a wave-shaped symbol; below it "3rd IF" ("3.ZF"), above it vertically "455 kHz". On the far right an arrow leads outwards from the last rectangle. All the main blocks lie in a horizontal row; the connections are drawn as arrows.
