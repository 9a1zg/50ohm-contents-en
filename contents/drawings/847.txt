1) Short description: Diagram of the electromagnetic spectrum with vertical scales for wavelength in metres and frequency in hertz; a narrow coloured bar marks "Light" between "Infrared" and "Ultraviolet".

2) Detailed description: The diagram is arranged vertically, but some of it is labelled as though it had to be turned through 90° to the left. Two parallel vertical arrows form the basic framework. The left arrow – labelled "λ [m]" – stands for the wavelength. It points upwards and carries marks from 10_–15 (bottom) to 10_6 (top). In addition the units of length "1 fm" (bottom) , "1 pm", "1 nm", "1 µm", "1 mm", "1 cm", "1 m", " 1 km" and "1 Mm" (top) are entered. The right arrow – labelled "f [Hz]" – gives the frequency. It points downwards and carries marks from 10_2 (top) to 10_23 (bottom). Here the frequency units "1 kHz" (top), "1 MHz", "1 GHz", "1 THz", "1 PHz", "1 EHz" and "1 ZHz" (bottom) are entered. Between the two arrows are the range names "Alternating currents" (top), "Radio waves", "Microwave radiation", "Radar", "Terahertz radiation", "Infrared", "Light", "Ultraviolet", "X-ray radiation", "Gamma radiation" and "Cosmic radiation" (bottom). At the height of "Light" there is a narrow rainbow-coloured stripe (red – orange – yellow – green – blue – violet). On the far left of the diagram this range, labelled "λ [nm]", is shown in detail between 380 (bottom) and 780 (top) over the full height.
