Brief summary: Block diagram of a radio station from left to right: TX → filter → 15 m line with two earth symbols → antenna tuner → SWR (meter) → antenna.

Details: On the left a square block labelled "TX", connected by an arrow to the right to a second square showing three wavy lines one above the other (filter representation, without text).
